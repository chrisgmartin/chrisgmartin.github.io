Forward Deployed Engineer — Data Engineering

A study guide for FDE-flavored Data Engineering roles at enterprise contract-intelligence and document-AI platforms. The Palantir-originated FDE archetype applied to embedded customer deployments — ingestion of messy contract / invoice / PO corpora, SLA-backed extraction accuracy, customer warehouse and ERP integration, and the consultant + engineer duality the role is built on. 17 interactive HTML chapters.

Open 00-START-HERE. Then read 03-fde-archetype and 04-customer-embedding — those framings are tested in every round. Drill 15-interview-questions. Reread 16-day-of the morning of.

Section B · FDE craft

What makes FDE distinct from a regular SWE or DE, what the round tests for, the three behavioral patterns that disqualify candidates. Critical 04 Customer Embedding Discovery, scoping, weekly cadence with the customer, the working agreement, kill criteria, escalation paths. The operating model the loop assumes you know. Critical
Section C · Data craft
05 Document Extraction Patterns for turning unstructured contracts / invoices / POs into structured data — OCR + NLP + CV, layout-aware models, human-in-the-loop, the eval loop that backs an SLA. 06 Pipelines Under Deployment Ingestion / ETL patterns when the customer's data shape changes weekly, schemas are negotiable, and "production" lives in the customer's cloud. Reprocessing, idempotency, versioned pipelines. 07 SQL & Contract Data Modeling The contract data model — parties, obligations, renewals, spend rollups, supplier hierarchies. SQL patterns over messy real-world contract data and the joins they imply. Critical 08 Warehouse & Integration Loading clean output into the customer's Snowflake / BigQuery, integrating with their ERP (SAP, Coupa, Oracle) and BI (Looker, Power BI, Tableau). Identity, auth, refresh cadence, backfills. 09 SLA-Backed Quality & Eval The differentiating piece. How to measure extraction quality, calibrate human-in-the-loop, ship to a contracted accuracy SLA, and surface failures to the customer before they do. Critical
Section D · Coding
10 Coding Fundamentals Python patterns FDE-DE rounds reach for — dict-heavy data shaping, generators for streaming, light algos, the "write something I can ship to a customer next week" bar. 11 Coding Problems Ten drillable problems — extraction-flavored cleaning, schema reshaping, fuzzy entity resolution, reconciliation, evaluation metrics. Multiple approaches per problem. Drill mode
Section E · Production / Cloud
12 Customer Integrations The enterprise stack you'll meet — Salesforce, SAP, Coupa, Workday, NetSuite, Snowflake, BigQuery, Looker. API shapes, auth patterns, refresh cadences, the political layer behind each one. 13 Ops & Rollout Embedded ops hygiene — staging environments at the customer, change management, runbooks, rollbacks, on-call when the customer's data team is your first responder.
