Practitioner playbook

The FDE-DE Playbook

The practitioner's operating manual once you're in role. 90-day plan, weekly cadence templates, working-agreement template, discovery question banks, kill-decision worksheets, runbook templates, escalation playbook, handoff checklist. Designed to be reused — copy, paste, adapt per deployment.

How to use this

This isn't a deep dive on theory — it's the bench of templates and procedures an FDE-DE actually runs. Skim once end-to-end so you know what's here; then pull individual chapters when you need them on a deployment. Most templates are designed to copy directly into Notion, Google Docs, or your team's wiki.
