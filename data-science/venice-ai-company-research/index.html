Pairing & caveats
Moving targets

Several facts here are company-reported or fast-moving: Venice's ARR is quoted between $70M and $100M and is unaudited; the VVV price and token emission schedule change constantly; some leadership names come from third-party aggregators. Where a number is load-bearing, the chapters flag the uncertainty. Re-verify live figures (pricing, model list, VVV price) before relying on them.
