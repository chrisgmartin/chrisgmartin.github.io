Senior Data Scientist, Business Analytics — Interview Prep

A study guide for the Senior Data Scientist, Business Analytics loop at a privacy-first, uncensored consumer-AI company — the kind of employer that runs on a freemium subscription, a usage-based API, and a crypto staking-for-compute token, and that deliberately doesn't log what its users type. It's a first-data-hire role reporting into business operations, and the interview turns on one question: how do you build a serious analytics function without collecting the data most companies live on? This guide is company-agnostic — it teaches the transferable spine so you can walk it into any employer of this shape.

The single reframe

The winning move is to treat the no-logging constraint not as a handicap but as the product: "we're not flying blind — we're flying on instruments, deliberately privacy-safe ones." Everything here builds toward being able to say that and back it with a real stack, real metrics, and a real 30-60-90. Start with 00-START-HERE. If you're targeting a specific employer, do your own company research alongside it.
