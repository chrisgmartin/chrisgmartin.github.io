Section A · Orient
00 Start Here Master index, study schedule, the single reframe that makes the rest of the guide click. 01 The Roles Decoded founding product DS and the Lead-DS role, side by side. What each team actually does, what the stack means, what the loop is really testing. 02 Positioning From Scratch Mindset before content — how to interview honestly when you don't have a direct analog on your résumé, and where to lean on adjacent experience.
Section B · Core Data Science
03 SQL for Product Analytics Window functions, cohort and funnel queries, retention curves, attribution, the gotchas interviewers love (NULLs, gaps-and-islands, dedupe). Critical 04 Experimentation Foundations A/B testing math, hypothesis tests, power and sample size, peeking, p-hacking, common pitfalls, and how to design an experiment from a fuzzy product question. Critical 05 Advanced Experimentation Multi-armed bandits, sequential testing, CUPED variance reduction, switchback tests, network effects and SUTVA violations, ratio metrics and the delta method. 06 Causal Inference When you can't randomize — difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, propensity scoring, regression discontinuity, synthetic controls. The the Lead-DS JD calls this out by name. Critical 07 Product Metrics North star, input vs output, leading vs lagging, guardrails, funnels, retention curves, sensitivity. How to design a metric and defend it under pressure. 08 Predictive Modeling for Business Forecasting, segmentation, propensity scoring, opportunity sizing. Models that drive sales, marketing, and finance decisions — not ML for its own sake. 09 Analytics Leadership Setting the technical bar, prioritizing across workstreams, building narrative from ambiguous questions, mentoring, and shipping insights leadership can act on. The the Lead-DS role bar.
Section C · Coding
10 Coding Fundamentals Python patterns DS interviewers reach for — dict-based aggregations, generators, pandas vs SQL, vectorization, sorting tricks, sliding window, Big-O cheat sheet. 11 Coding & SQL Problems Mixed drill set — Python data-manipulation problems and SQL puzzles (funnel, cohort retention, sessionization, gaps-and-islands). Multiple approaches per problem, drill mode hides solutions. Drill mode
Section D · Production / Cloud
12 Defining the Data Stack Greenfield problem — how to define the experimentation, warehouse, and event-tracking stack at an early-stage company. The "lay the foundation" prompt, answered. 13 Dashboards & BI Self-serve principles, Looker / Mode / Hex patterns, metric layers, dashboard hygiene, the "vanity vs decision" distinction stakeholders never name but always feel.
