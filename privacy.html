Privacy

Field Guides has no accounts, no forms, no ads and no cookies. This page says exactly what does happen when you read it, and who else is involved in getting the pages to you.

The short version

  • I don't ask for, collect or store any personal information. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • The site sets no cookies. A few reading preferences are kept in your own browser and never sent anywhere.
  • Visits are counted with a privacy-first, cookieless analytics tool. It does not identify you or follow you to other sites.
  • Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, because there is nothing to sell.

What stays in your browser

To remember your choices between visits, the site writes a handful of values to your browser's localStorage, all prefixed fg: — light or dark theme, drill-mode settings, and which checklist items and practice questions you have ticked off. They live only on your device, are never transmitted to me or anyone else, and disappear if you clear site data in your browser. Because they are strictly functional and never leave your machine, there is no cookie banner.

Analytics

On christopherm.xyz I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which guides are read and whether pages load quickly. It uses no cookies and no localStorage, does not fingerprint your browser or track you across sites, and gives me aggregate figures only: page views, referrers, country, browser and device type, and load-time measurements. I cannot see who you are and I do not try to.

Fonts, styles and every other script are served from this site itself. There are no advertising networks, social widgets or embedded trackers.

Links to other sites

Guides link out to documentation, papers, source repositories and, from the support card, to Ko-fi. Once you follow a link you are on someone else's site under their privacy policy; if you choose to support this work, the payment provider — not me — handles your payment details. The site sends only its origin (not the page you were on) as the referrer to other sites.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the stored preferences at any time through your browser's site-data settings, and the site works with them cleared or blocked. Content blockers that stop the analytics script do not affect anything else. Because I hold no personal data about readers, there is nothing for me to look up, correct or delete — but if you are in a jurisdiction that gives you rights over personal data (such as the GDPR or CCPA) and you have a question or concern, raise it and I will answer.

Contact and changes

Questions about this page: open an issue on the site's GitHub repository. If what the site does changes — a new tool, a new provider — this page changes first, and the date at the top with it. See also the terms of use.
