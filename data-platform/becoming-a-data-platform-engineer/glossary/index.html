Glossary & Cheat Sheets

The reference to keep open the whole way through. A plain-language glossary of every term the curriculum uses — each cross-linked to the course that teaches it — plus quick cheat sheets for SQL, the command line, the data stack, and the decisions you'll make most often. When a word is fuzzy, this is where you look.

Bookmark this

This isn't a course to "complete" — it's a companion. From your very first chapter, when you hit an unfamiliar term (CDC? idempotent? lakehouse?), come here, get the one-line definition, and follow the pointer to where it's taught in depth. Use your browser's find (Ctrl/⌘-F) on the glossary page to jump straight to a term.

How to use it

Two complementary references:

  • The Glossary answers "what does this word mean?" — reach for it any time a term trips you up, in any course.
  • The Cheat Sheets answer "what's the syntax / which option do I pick?" — reach for them while you're building, in the craft, tooling, and capstone courses.

Both link back into the courses, so the reference and the teaching stay connected.

About this guide. Course 8 of the Data Platform curriculum — the reference layer. It supports every other course; there's no need to read it front-to-back. For the senior view of how the concepts fit together, see the Systems Design reference.
