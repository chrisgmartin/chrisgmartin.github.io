Start Here & Roadmap

Before you learn anything technical, get the map. This short course explains what a Data Platform Engineer actually does, lays out the eight-course curriculum and how the pieces ladder together, gives you a realistic six-month schedule, and shows you how to study so the effort sticks. Start here — it will save you weeks.

The one promise of this curriculum

If you can write a SELECT with a JOIN and a small Python script, you have enough to begin. By the end you will have built and operated a real (if small) data platform, and the senior Data Platform Systems Design guide will read like a colleague talking, not a foreign language.
