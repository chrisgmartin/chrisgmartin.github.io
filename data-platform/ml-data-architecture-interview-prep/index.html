ML Data Architecture — Interview Prep

Company-agnostic prep for senior and staff loops where the job is the data behind machine learning — ML platform, data engineering for ML, applied-ML infrastructure. A five-move method that structures any ML data design question, five whiteboard drills, the decision matrices that win the trade-off conversation, graded practice questions, and day-of tactics.

How to read this guide — the method

These rounds are graded on whether you can hold the offline and online paths in your head at once and say exactly where they diverge — that is where real systems break. The five moves in 02 force that structure; the drills in Section B are the questions you are most likely to be asked; 08 gives you the matrices to defend every fork. The underlying material lives in the ML Data Architecture deep dive.
