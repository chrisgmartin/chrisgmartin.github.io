OODA Loops — Case Study Prep

How to work a one-page operational case as a loop: write the current state as facts with sources, write the intended state with a date, name each gap with its cause, and close it with actions that carry their own measurement. Three worked cases on GridDP's fleet, the five operating principles one GPU builder says its interviews test for, and a minute plan for the forty-five minutes.

If you only have an hour

Read 02 for the three moves, then work 06 with a timer before reading its answer, then 10 for the plan. That is enough to be problem-first in the room. The rest is why it works and two more cases to practise on.

Study pathsPick the plan that fits the time you have

In an afternoon

01 → 05, then all three cases. The loop as Boyd drew it, each stage taken apart, and the method run three times.

The day before

09 and 10, then one case aloud with a fifteen-minute timer. Stories in loop form, the plan for the room, one rehearsal.

Section A · The loop
00 Start Here The forty-five-minute interview this guide prepares for, what the interviewers say they look for, and the GridDP fleet the cases use. 01 The Loop Boyd Drew Observe, orient, decide, act: the real sketch, why orientation is the big box, and what tempo means for an operator. 02 Current State, Intended State, Gap The interview's version of the loop: say what is, say what should be and by when, and name each difference with a cause.
Section B · Work the loop
03 Observe Like an Operator Reading a primer for facts, sources and ages; separating measurements from claims; the questions to ask in the first five minutes. 04 Orient: First Principles and Constraints Where the thinking happens: constraints from physics and contracts, the one bottleneck, and hypotheses with the evidence that would kill them. 05 Decide and Act Options including doing nothing, one-way doors, the cheapest probe that changes your mind, and closing the loop with a measurement.
Section C · Three cases
06 Case: The November Delivery Slip Sixty-four servers promised, forty on site, three weeks left. The loop run end to end on a supply problem. 07 Case: Three Counts of One Fleet NetBox, finance and telemetry disagree about how many servers are deployed. A data problem worked as a gap to close. 08 Case: Failures Double at One Site Reno's GPU failure rate doubles in a month. Is it real, what explains it, and which probe to run first.
Section D · The room
09 Five Principles and a Mission What the company says it is for and how it says it operates, what each principle is testing, and how to tell a story that shows it. 10 The Forty-Five Minutes A minute plan for the conversation: problem first, think aloud, apply the loop to your own work, ask back, and close.

About this guide. Interview prep for a behavioural-plus-case round at a GPU data-centre builder, written in November 2026. The loop is Boyd's, drawn as he drew it; the cases are GridDP teaching fixtures; the company's mission and principles are quoted from its public hiring pages and linked. It pairs with Building an Operational Ontology, which designs the system that would answer the cases' questions, and with the Fluidstack profile.
