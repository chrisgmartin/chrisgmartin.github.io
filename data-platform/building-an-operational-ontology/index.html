Building an Operational Ontology

How to model a company's machines, orders, sites, promises and incidents as one set of objects and links over a dozen systems that disagree, give each thing one identity, keep every fact sourced, fresh and permissioned, and let people and agents act through it. GridDP's fleet is the worked example, drawn in full.

Read 01 for what the thing is, 02 for the whole model on one page, and 06 for how permissions survive a join. Those three are the shape of the job. The rest is how to keep it true and make it operational.

About this guide. A deep dive for data platform engineers asked to build the internal model of record for a company whose operations live in a dozen tools, so that people and agents can act on fresh, traceable, permissioned facts. It is a modelling guide first; the pipelines underneath are linked, not re-taught. Written in November 2026 with primary sources throughout. GridDP's figures are teaching fixtures.
