About Field Guides

Why this site exists, who it is for, and who writes it.

What this is

Field Guides is long-form writing on how to build real systems, for people who already do the work: senior engineers, data and product people across AI, data, DeFi and software. I write each guide from the practitioner's seat, with one running example carried the whole way through and the trade-offs named rather than glossed.

There is no textbook neutrality here. Where I have a view, I say so, and I say when it does not hold.

Who writes it

I'm Chris. I build data and AI products, and the guides come out of that work. They cover the problems I have actually had to solve, not a tour of the manual. When I learn something the hard way, it tends to end up here.
