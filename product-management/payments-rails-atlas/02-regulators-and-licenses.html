✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. You want to take USD from users in 49 states and DC. What does FinCEN MSB registration give you, and what does it not?

    Show answer

    It is the federal BSA and AML registration, and it is the easy part. It does not give you permission to transmit money in any state. That is the state money transmitter licence, held state by state, plus a BitLicense if you serve New York. See licence types.

  2. A US exchange plans a Pix on-ramp in Brazil. How can it reach the rail, and what separate licence applies to the crypto side?

    Show answer

    Direct Pix participation is for banks and payment institutions, so it would either qualify as a direct participant or use an indirect arrangement through one. Dealing in crypto then needs its own authorisation under the BACEN framework. Rail access and crypto permission are separate layers. See licence needed per rail.

  3. A company already holds a MiCA CASP authorisation. Why might it still need an EMI or payment institution licence?

    Show answer

    CASP covers crypto-asset services across the EU. Holding customer fiat balances or initiating payments is a payment-services matter, so it needs an EMI or PI, or a partner who holds one. See crypto licensing and licence types.

  4. A user of your Dublin entity sends €400 of USDC to an exchange in Hong Kong. Does the Travel Rule apply on your side?

    Show answer

    Yes. The EU rule has no minimum, in force since December 2024, so originator and beneficiary data must travel with the transfer even though the Hong Kong threshold is HK$8,000. Build to the strictest rule that applies to your own entity. See Travel Rule grid.

Takeaway

  • Licensing is two stacks: the right to move fiat on a rail, and the right to deal in crypto.
  • Direct rail participation is limited to banks and licensed institutions, so most integrators reach a rail through a sponsor and inherit its limits.
  • The US needs FinCEN MSB registration, state licences and, in New York, a BitLicense; the federal registration is the easy part.
  • A MiCA CASP authorisation passports across the EU and replaces national VASP regimes, but holding customer fiat still needs an EMI or PI.
  • Travel Rule thresholds run from zero in the EU and Japan to HK$8,000 in Hong Kong, so build to the strictest one that applies to you.
  • Treat every figure in these tables as a dated starting point and confirm with the regulator before you commit.
