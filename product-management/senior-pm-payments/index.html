Senior PM — Payments (Emerging Markets)

Senior-level interview prep for a Payments PM owning fiat rails across high-growth and emerging markets — UPI, IMPS, PIX, SEPA Instant, local card schemes, Open Banking — with deep partner-orchestration, fraud, and conversion responsibility.

If you only have an hour

Open 00-START-HERE first. Then drill 15-interview-questions with drill mode on. Reread 16-day-of the morning of.

  • Day 2: 04, 05 (the two deep dives)
  • Day 3: 06, 07 (patterns + evals)
  • Day 4: 08, 09 (error handling + governance)
