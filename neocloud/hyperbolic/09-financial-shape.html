Section D · Position

Financial Shape

Hyperbolic is private and Series-A stage. Financial picture is less developed than mature competitors. The economic profile is software-platform-like more than capital-intensive-cloud-like.

Funding history

A seed round and a Series A closed in 2024, and the company's blog put total funding at $20M in December 2024. On 27 October 2026 Hyperbolic announced a $30M Series A1 led by Walden Catalyst, with Dell Technologies Capital, General Catalyst, Vista Equity Partners, Cambium Capital and Salience Capital. Further rounds are likely as the company scales.

Valuation

The company has not disclosed a valuation for the A1 round. A nine-figure valuation was reported for the earlier round, and this profile could not verify it against a primary source. Any figure is likely well below Together's or Vast's.

Revenue picture

Revenue is not audited or filed. The CEO wrote in October 2026 that the company ended 2025 near $15M annualised and is on track for $150M annualised by the end of 2026. The second figure is a target, not a result.

Trajectory

Growth trajectory through 2024-2026 has been steady. The company hasn't pursued aggressive capital deployment; growth is more organic. That was the 2025 view. A move from about $15M to a $150M target in one year would not be steady, so watch whether the A1 funds that jump.

  4. Is steady growth with restrained capital deployment a strength or a weakness here?

Takeaway

  • Hyperbolic closed a seed round and Series A in 2024 and a $30M Series A1 in October 2026, with further rounds likely as it scales.
  • The A1 valuation is undisclosed, and an earlier nine-figure figure is unverified.
  • Revenue is not audited. The CEO cites about $15M annualised at the end of 2025 and a $150M target for the end of 2026.
  • Growth has been steady and organic, with capital deployed carefully rather than aggressively.
