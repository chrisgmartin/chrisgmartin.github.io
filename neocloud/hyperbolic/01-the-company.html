Section A · The company

The Company

Hyperbolic is a young AI infrastructure company founded by AI researchers. The company runs both a GPU marketplace and a managed inference service. Series A scale; PhD-founder credibility; smaller absolute footprint than mature competitors but growing.

Founding (2023)

Hyperbolic was founded in 2023, in the immediate wake of the ChatGPT-driven AI demand surge. The founding context — open-access, decentralized AI infrastructure — placed Hyperbolic in a similar conceptual neighborhood to Together.AI but with a distinct emphasis on combining marketplace and inference.

Founders

Hyperbolic's founders have PhD backgrounds in AI, math, and computer science. The technical credibility is comparable to Together.AI's founders, though at a smaller commercial scale.

The founder profile signals: this is a company that will ship research-credible product even if it doesn't have a CoreWeave-scale infrastructure budget.

Original thesis

The original thesis blends two ideas:

  1. GPU compute should be accessible and affordable. A marketplace model can pool supply efficiently.
  2. Open-source-model inference is a real platform opportunity. Managed inference on curated models complements the raw GPU rental.

The combination is distinctive. Most companies in the neocloud landscape pick one or the other; Hyperbolic deliberately runs both, smaller in scale than the leaders in either category but with the dual-product story.

Funding

Hyperbolic raised seed and Series A funding through 2023-2024. Total capital raised through 2025 is in the multi-tens-of-millions range — meaningful but smaller than Together / Fireworks at comparable stages.

Scale today

Hyperbolic's scale through 2025-2026:

  • Active GPU marketplace with thousands of GPUs available.
  • Managed inference service across the major open-source models.
  • Customer base of indie developers, AI startups, and researchers.
  • Revenue not officially disclosed; likely in the mid-eight-figure range based on traffic and market position.

Smaller than Together, RunPod, or Vast — but real and growing.

Culture

Hyperbolic's posture is engineering-led with strong open-source ecosystem engagement. The team publishes research, contributes to open-source projects, and engages actively with the AI developer community. Less commercial-marketing-heavy than RunPod; more research-credibility-oriented than Vast.

Takeaway

Hyperbolic is a Series-A-stage company in the neocloud landscape with a dual marketplace-and-inference identity. The next chapter unpacks that two-product thesis.
