Section B · The inference service

The Inference Service

Hyperbolic's inference service offers per-token API access to curated open-source models. Smaller catalog and traffic than Together; competitive on price; growing.

Inference API

Standard OpenAI-API-compatible inference endpoints. Customers point their OpenAI client at Hyperbolic's endpoint and run.

Model catalog

Curated open-source models including:

  • Llama family.
  • DeepSeek variants.
  • Qwen.
  • Mixtral / Mistral.
  • Other selected open-source releases.

Catalog is smaller than Together's. The selection prioritizes the highest-traffic models.

Per-token pricing

Pricing is competitive with the broader open-source inference category. Often comparable to Together's or slightly below for specific models. The smaller scale means Hyperbolic has less serving optimization headroom but also lower cost overhead.

vs Together / Fireworks

  • Together and Fireworks are larger by traffic.
  • Hyperbolic's research credibility and dual-product story are unique.
  • Per-token pricing and quality are competitive on most overlapping models.

Position in inference category

Hyperbolic is a credible second-tier player in the managed-inference category. Specific use cases (cost-sensitive inference, ease of integrating with raw-GPU rental) bring customers; the broader category leader pull means Hyperbolic doesn't dominate.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. What does an OpenAI-compatible endpoint save a customer, and what does it not guarantee?

    Show answer

    It saves client changes, because you point an OpenAI client at Hyperbolic's endpoint and run. It does not guarantee the same models, quality, latency or rate limits. Test the models you need on the target service. See inference API.

  2. Why would a provider keep a smaller catalog than Together?

    Show answer

    It concentrates serving work on the highest-traffic models, where tuning pays back fastest. The trade is breadth. A team that needs a niche model may find it missing. See model catalog.

  3. The profile says smaller scale means less optimisation headroom but lower cost overhead. Explain why prices can still be comparable.

    Show answer

    A large provider spreads serving optimisation across far more traffic, so it can cut cost per token further. A small provider carries less overhead, which offsets some of that gap. The result is per-token prices near Together's, or slightly below on specific models. See per-token pricing.

  4. A team needs one popular open model and one rare one in the same product. How do you decide where to buy?

    Show answer

    Check the catalog before price. On overlapping models the profile finds price and quality competitive, so coverage decides. If the rare model is missing, use a larger catalog such as Together's or serve it from rented GPUs. See vs Together / Fireworks.

  5. When is a second-tier inference provider the right pick over the category leader?

    Show answer

    When cost-sensitive inference or easy pairing with raw GPU rental matters more than catalog breadth and traffic scale. The leader wins when you need the widest model choice or the strongest research visibility. See position in the category.

Takeaway

  • Hyperbolic offers OpenAI-compatible inference endpoints, so customers switch by changing the client's base URL and key.
  • The catalog is curated and smaller than Together's, weighted to the highest-traffic open models.
  • Per-token pricing is competitive, often near Together's or slightly below on specific models.
  • Together and Fireworks are larger by traffic, so Hyperbolic has less optimisation headroom but also lower cost overhead.
  • The service is a competent second-tier player, chosen for cost and for the link to raw GPU rental.
