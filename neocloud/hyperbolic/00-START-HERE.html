Eleven-chapter profile of Hyperbolic — open-access AI cloud and inference platform. Series A startup with PhD-founder credibility. Marketplace + managed inference dual product at smaller scale than the named leaders.

Scope & audience

Hyperbolic's positioning at the intersection of GPU marketplaces (Vast, TensorDock) and managed inference (Together.AI, Fireworks). Why the dual product, where the differentiation comes from, customer segments, scale, and outlook.

Key framings to carry

  1. The two-product thesis is the strategic crux. Marketplace gives raw GPU access; inference packages it. Same supply base; different commercial layers. Whether this dual surface works at Hyperbolic's scale is the question.
  2. Founder credibility is the early moat. PhD-founded; AI / math / CS lineage. Sets a research-quality bar that translates to product credibility.
  3. Scale ceiling is the bear case. Smaller capital base than CoreWeave, Crusoe, Together; longer path to enterprise scale.

01 → 02 (the two-product thesis — load-bearing) → 03–04 (the two products) → 05–06 (infra, customers) → 07–10 (strengths, positioning, outlook).
