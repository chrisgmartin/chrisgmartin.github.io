Section C · Commercial

Major Customer Deals

Nebius signed substantial multi-year capacity commitments with Microsoft (about $17.4B over five years) and two agreements with Meta (about $3B, and up to about $27B). These anchor commitments transformed the company's commercial profile.

The Microsoft deal

On 8 September 2025, Nebius agreed a multi-year capacity commitment with Microsoft. The Form 6-K states a five-year term and a total contract value of about $17.4B through 2031, which could reach about $19.4B if Microsoft buys additional capacity. The deal:

  • Five-year term, running through 2031.
  • Dedicated GPU capacity at a new data center in Vineland, New Jersey.
  • Capacity delivered in tranches during 2025 and 2026.

The deal validated Nebius as a strategic supplier to a hyperscaler — similar in structure to CoreWeave's Microsoft relationship but with Nebius as the supplier.

Meta and others

Nebius has signed two agreements with Meta. On 11 November 2025, with its third quarter results, it announced an agreement valued at about $3 billion over five years. On 16 March 2026 it announced a second five-year agreement worth up to about $27 billion. Of that, $12 billion is dedicated capacity across several sites, built on one of the first large-scale deployments of NVIDIA Vera Rubin, with deliveries from early 2027. Meta can commit up to $15 billion more over the five years for capacity from later clusters. The $27 billion is a ceiling on contract value, not a guaranteed purchase. The fiscal 2025 Form 20-F calls both Meta and Microsoft "multi-year, multi-billion dollar agreements" and does not give revenue shares.

Multiple anchor customers reduce the concentration risk of relying on a single relationship.

Contract shapes

The major deals share characteristics:

  • Multi-year reserved capacity commitments.
  • Custom pricing reflecting the scale.
  • Capacity delivered as Nebius's buildouts complete.
  • Bespoke operational terms.

Implications

The customer commitments transform Nebius:

  • Forward revenue visibility extends into the late 2020s.
  • The company joins CoreWeave in the small group of neoclouds with hyperscaler-anchor relationships.
  • Public-market valuation reflects the contract value.
  • Capital deployment is justified by the committed revenue.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. What did the Microsoft agreement change about Nebius's position, beyond the dollar figure?

    Show answer

    It made Nebius a supplier to a hyperscaler and gave it forward revenue visibility into the late 2020s. That, in turn, justified capital deployment against committed revenue. See the Microsoft deal and implications.

  2. The Form 6-K states a contract value of about $17.4B. Why should an analyst not treat that as revenue booked?

    Show answer

    Capacity is delivered in stages as Nebius builds, and payment depends on deployment and availability of the service. The figure is a ceiling on contracted value, so revenue follows delivery. See contract shapes.

  3. Why does having Meta and others matter for how you read the Microsoft deal?

    Show answer

    Several anchor customers reduce concentration risk. If one softens or restructures, the others still carry the build. Meta has two agreements, about $3B and up to about $27B, but the larger one is a ceiling, so weigh what is dedicated ($12B) apart from what is optional. See Meta and others.

  4. A reserved-capacity customer with a small commitment asks for Microsoft's terms. What do you tell them?

    Show answer

    Microsoft's terms are custom: multi-year, bespoke pricing and operational terms, scaled to the commitment. Smaller customers commit on shorter terms with ordinary discounts. See contract shapes.

  5. How is Nebius's position with Microsoft the same as CoreWeave's and how does it differ?

    Show answer

    Both are suppliers of dedicated GPU capacity to a hyperscaler under long contracts. The chapter notes the structure is similar; Nebius is the supplier here, with a smaller scale and a European base. See the Microsoft deal.

Takeaway

  • In September 2025 Nebius signed a five-year Microsoft commitment of about $17.4B through 2031, delivered in stages as capacity is built.
  • The deal makes Nebius a strategic supplier to a hyperscaler, in the same structure as CoreWeave's Microsoft relationship.
  • Two Meta agreements, about $3B and up to about $27B, reduce the concentration risk of leaning on one customer.
  • These contracts are reserved, custom-priced and delivered as the buildout completes, so revenue lags the signing.
  • Forward visibility justifies the capital deployment, and the public valuation reflects the contract value.
