Section D · Position

Customers

Nebius's customer base spans hyperscaler-tier commitments through European enterprise to self-serve developers. The Microsoft / Meta-tier deals dominate the strategic narrative.

Anchor customers

Microsoft and Meta-tier customers are the strategic anchors covered in chapter 04.

European AI labs and enterprises

Named customers announced by Nebius in 2025 and 2026 include Black Forest Labs, Prosus and bunq, and Revolut and Sword Health appear on the Token Factory page. In September 2026 Palantir named Nebius its preferred sovereign AI infrastructure partner. European customers value Nebius's data-residency posture:

  • European AI labs and research institutions.
  • European enterprises with EU-data-residency requirements.
  • European governmental and quasi-public organizations.

Self-serve customers

Smaller developers and AI companies access Nebius through self-serve account creation. The customer experience is good but the segment is smaller than at competitors more focused on indie / developer customers.

Hyperscaler-as-customer dynamic

Like CoreWeave, Nebius treats hyperscalers as customers. Nebius sells dedicated GPU capacity to Microsoft under the contract described in chapter 04. This is a strategic posture, not a competitive one.

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. Why do European buyers pick Nebius, according to the chapter, and which three kinds of buyer does that cover?

    Show answer

    Its data-residency posture. It covers European AI labs and research institutions, enterprises with EU-residency rules, and governmental or quasi-public organisations. See European AI labs and enterprises.

  2. Why is the self-serve segment smaller at Nebius than at developer-focused competitors, even though the experience is good?

    Show answer

    Strategy and focus. Nebius concentrates on anchor and enterprise customers, while the developer-focused clouds build for indie buyers. A good product does not create a segment by itself. See self-serve customers.

  3. Microsoft is both a customer and a competitor in cloud. How does the chapter resolve that?

    Show answer

    It treats the relationship as strategic, not competitive: Nebius sells dedicated GPU capacity to Microsoft. CoreWeave takes the same stance. See hyperscaler-as-customer dynamic.

  4. A US start-up with no residency needs asks if Nebius is a fit. What do you weigh?

    Show answer

    The self-serve experience is good, so it can work. But the European advantage is irrelevant to them, so compare price, availability and tooling with developer-focused peers. See self-serve customers.

  5. Your forecast leans on the Microsoft and Meta contracts. What is the concentration risk and how would you check it?

    Show answer

    A few anchor customers dominate the revenue story. Read the quarterly concentration disclosures to see what share each one holds. See anchor customers.

Takeaway

  • The anchor customers are Microsoft and Meta-tier buyers, covered in chapter 04.
  • European labs, enterprises and public bodies buy Nebius for its data-residency posture.
  • Self-serve developers can use Nebius, but that segment is smaller than at developer-focused peers.
  • Hyperscalers are customers, not rivals: Nebius sells dedicated capacity to Microsoft.
  • Revenue concentration in a few buyers is the main thing to monitor.
