Section B · Operations

Infrastructure

Nebius's footprint is anchored in Europe but expanding into the US. The infrastructure quality reflects the Yandex engineering heritage — operationally strong, well-engineered, comparable to other top-tier neoclouds.

European footprint

Nebius's primary datacenter footprint is in Europe, with sites including Finland and other locations chosen for energy availability and cooling efficiency. As of November 2026 the public regions are Finland, France, Spain, the UK, Israel and two US sites, Kansas City in Missouri and Woodbury in Minnesota. An Iceland region is private, for existing deployments only.

European anchoring is strategic: it gives Nebius data-residency credentials that matter for EU enterprise customers and aligns the company with the broader European AI sovereignty conversation.

US expansion

Nebius has expanded into US datacenters through 2024-2026 to support US customer commitments (Microsoft and others). The US footprint supplements the European base. The Microsoft contract is served from a new data center in Vineland, New Jersey, according to the Form 6-K of 8 September 2025.

GPU mix

  • H100 and H200: still on offer, for example in Finland, France and Kansas City.
  • Blackwell: B200 and B300 HGX in several regions, plus GB200 NVL72 and GB300 NVL72 systems, as listed in the fiscal 2025 Form 20-F. Vera Rubin NVL72 is announced for US and Europe customers.
  • NVIDIA reference architecture configurations including the GB200 NVL72 systems for large training clusters.
  • RTX PRO 6000 in Madrid, Kansas City and the UK. The 20-F does not name A100, so treat any A100 as legacy.

The GPU mix matches what other top-tier neoclouds run. The NVIDIA strategic relationship supports allocation.

Network fabric

InfiniBand-based cluster fabric supporting multi-thousand-GPU training cluster sizes. NVIDIA reference designs throughout. The engineering quality of the network deployment is one of the technical strengths Nebius leverages.

Scaling pace

Scaling pace through 2024-2026 has been aggressive: multiple new sites, large GPU procurement, customer-commitment-driven capacity additions. The fiscal 2025 Form 20-F counts more than 16 data center locations and more than 2 GW of contracted power as of February 2026, with about 170 MW active at 31 December 2025.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. Why would Nebius place a site in Finland, and what does the European base give it that the power bill alone does not?

    Show answer

    Sites are chosen for energy availability and cooling efficiency. The European anchor also gives data-residency credentials for EU enterprises and fits the sovereignty conversation, which US-only peers cannot offer. See European footprint.

  2. A customer wants a 4,000-GPU training run. What in the chapter supports Nebius hosting it, and what would you still ask to see?

    Show answer

    InfiniBand fabric on NVIDIA reference designs supports multi-thousand-GPU clusters, and GB200 NVL72 systems target large training. Still ask how many GPUs of that type are deliverable in one region by your start date, because capacity arrives in tranches as allocation lands. See network fabric.

  3. The chapter says the GPU mix matches other top-tier neoclouds. What does Nebius's NVIDIA relationship add beyond that?

    Show answer

    Allocation. New generations are supply-limited, and a strategic relationship helps Nebius get Blackwell parts as they arrive. The mix is the same on paper, but the delivery date is what the relationship moves. See GPU mix.

  4. Why did Nebius build US datacenters, and what does that say about the order of signing and building?

    Show answer

    The US sites exist to serve US customer commitments such as Microsoft's. Contracts come first and capacity follows, which lowers the utilisation risk of the buildout compared with building on speculation. See US expansion and scaling pace.

  5. Two neoclouds offer the same GPUs and the same InfiniBand fabric. What decides between them for an EU enterprise?

    Show answer

    Location and posture: where the data lives, whether the provider can speak to sovereignty requirements, and the compliance paperwork. Hardware parity removes performance as the differentiator, so residency becomes the tie-breaker. See European footprint.

Takeaway

  • Nebius's base is European, with sites such as Finland chosen for energy and cooling, and a US footprint added since 2024.
  • The European anchor gives it data-residency credentials that matter to EU enterprises.
  • The GPU mix is H100 and H200 at scale, Blackwell as allocation arrives, plus some legacy A100.
  • Cluster networking is InfiniBand on NVIDIA reference designs, enough for multi-thousand-GPU training.
  • Growth is driven by customer commitments, so capacity is built against signed demand.
