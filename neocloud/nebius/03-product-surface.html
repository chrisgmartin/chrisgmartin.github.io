Section B · Operations

Product Surface

Nebius's product extends beyond raw GPU rental into managed AI services. The breadth reflects the engineering depth available — building managed platforms takes the kind of engineering team Yandex left behind.

GPU compute

The core product is GPU compute in dedicated and reserved configurations. On-demand for shorter workloads; reserved for committed.

Managed Kubernetes

Nebius operates a managed Kubernetes offering tuned for AI workloads. The Yandex engineering heritage includes deep distributed systems expertise; the K8s offering reflects that.

AI Studio and Token Factory

Nebius AI Studio is now Nebius Token Factory. Nebius announced the new name on 5 November 2025 as "the next evolution of Nebius AI Studio," and AI Studio users were upgraded automatically. The old AI Studio address now leads to Token Factory.

Token Factory targets customers who want a more managed experience than raw GPU access. It combines serverless inference, dedicated endpoints, fine-tuning and team controls such as SSO and unified billing, with OpenAI-compatible APIs.

Inference

Token Factory is the inference product. It competes with Together and Fireworks for some workloads. The product surface keeps changing: in October 2026 Nebius announced the acquisition of Inferize to strengthen the Token Factory inference stack.

Storage and supporting services

  • Object storage for AI workload data.
  • Parallel filesystem options for high-throughput training.
  • VPC and networking services.
  • Identity and access management.

Developer experience

The DX is engineering-led and well-built. Console, CLI, API, SDKs in major languages. The product feels more "real cloud" than competing neoclouds with thinner product surfaces.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. Why is Nebius's product surface described as closer to a small hyperscaler than to a GPU-rental specialist?

    Show answer

    It sells managed Kubernetes, a managed AI service layer, inference, object and parallel storage, networking and identity, not only GPU hours. That breadth lets one customer buy the whole stack. See developer experience.

  2. A team trains with Slurm and has no Kubernetes skills. Which part of the product surface fits them, and what is the trade-off?

    Show answer

    Raw GPU compute with reserved or on-demand clusters, which gives the Slurm workflow they know. The trade-off is that they operate more themselves than they would on a managed service. See GPU compute and managed Kubernetes.

  3. Who is Token Factory (formerly AI Studio) aimed at, and who should not buy it?

    Show answer

    Customers who want a more managed experience than raw GPU access, with compute, inference and tools bundled. A team that needs full control of drivers, scheduling and topology should rent compute directly. See AI Studio and Token Factory.

  4. The chapter says inference competes with Together and Fireworks "for some workloads." Which workloads, and which would you leave out?

    Show answer

    Those where a managed endpoint is enough and the buyer is already on Nebius for compute. Leave out buyers who choose on model catalogue breadth or per-token price alone, since the inference surface is still evolving. See inference.

  5. You are pricing a migration of a training job from a thin-surface GPU cloud. Which of Nebius's supporting services lowers the migration cost, and why?

    Show answer

    Object storage and parallel filesystem options carry datasets and checkpoints, and VPC with IAM carries the security model. Without them you rebuild that plumbing yourself. See storage and supporting services.

Takeaway

  • The core product is GPU compute, on demand for short work and reserved for committed work.
  • Managed Kubernetes tuned for AI workloads reflects the distributed-systems depth of the Yandex engineers.
  • Token Factory, the renamed AI Studio, and the inference platform serve buyers who want managed services rather than raw GPUs.
  • Storage, networking and identity services make Nebius feel like a real cloud, not a rental shop.
  • The developer experience is engineering-led, with console, CLI, API and SDKs.
