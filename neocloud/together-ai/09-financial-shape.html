Section D · Position

Financial Shape

Together is private; financial detail is limited. Funding history and reported valuations are the main public anchors. The economic profile differs from capital-intensive GPU clouds — software margins matter more, GPU cap-ex less.

Funding history

Public funding rounds, approximate amounts:

  • Seed in 2022.
  • Series A in 2023.
  • Series B in 2024 (~$100M+).
  • Subsequent rounds in 2024-2025 supporting infrastructure expansion.

Total equity capital raised is in the several-hundred-million range. Investors include strategic VCs and infrastructure-focused funds.

Valuation

  • Series B valuation reportedly in the low billions.
  • Subsequent rounds at higher valuations.
  • Together is now a unicorn-tier company by valuation.

Revenue picture

Specific revenue numbers aren't public. Reasonable inferences:

  • Annualized run-rate in the meaningful nine-figure range by 2025-2026.
  • Heavy growth on per-token API traffic.
  • Meaningful share from enterprise dedicated commitments.
  • Training and fine-tuning revenue secondary but real.

Unit economics

The per-token unit economics depend on:

  • Inference cost (GPU time, electricity).
  • Serving optimization that determines tokens-per-second per GPU.
  • Effective utilization across the GPU fleet.
  • Customer-paid revenue per token.

Together's research-driven optimization compresses cost-per-token below less-optimized competitors. Margins on per-token revenue are reasonable; gross margins compress as competitors converge on similar optimizations.

Growth trajectory

Together's growth rate through 2024-2026 has been strong but not the order-of-magnitude pace CoreWeave saw. The per-token model scales linearly with customer adoption rather than mechanically with infrastructure deployment. Customer acquisition and traffic growth are the constraints.

Path to profitability

The path to sustainable profitability requires:

  • Sufficient gross margin on per-token revenue.
  • Scaling operating costs (R&D, sales, support) sub-linearly with revenue.
  • Continued growth in customer base and traffic.

The model is more software-like than capital-intensive-GPU-cloud-like. Profitability should follow as scale builds, provided per-token margins hold.

Takeaway

Together's financial profile is software-platform-like rather than infrastructure-investment-like. Less capital-intensive than CoreWeave / Crusoe; more revenue-growth-dependent than capex-bound. The next chapter reads the outlook.
