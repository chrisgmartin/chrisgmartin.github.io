Section A · Origin

The Company

Together.AI was founded in 2022 by AI researchers (including a co-founder behind FlashAttention) on a thesis that decentralized, open-source-friendly inference and training infrastructure could be a strategic alternative to closed-source proprietary platforms.

Founding (2022)

Together.AI was founded in 2022 by Vipul Ved Prakash, Ce Zhang, Chris Re, Percy Liang, and Tri Dao — a group whose mix of academic research credibility (Stanford, including FlashAttention's Tri Dao) and entrepreneurial experience set the company's strategic posture from day one.

The founding timing matters. 2022 was when proprietary LLMs were starting to dominate (GPT-4 era), and the team bet that the open-source ecosystem (Llama, later Mixtral, Qwen, DeepSeek, and by 2026 Kimi, GLM and MiniMax) would matter enough commercially to support a platform business.

Founders

  • Vipul Ved Prakash — CEO. Background in entrepreneurship and ML / data infrastructure (Topsy founder, sold to Apple). Drives the commercial strategy.
  • Ce Zhang — CTO. ETH Zurich professor. Distributed systems and ML systems research.
  • Chris Ré — Stanford professor; co-founder. Deep ML systems and weak-supervision research lineage.
  • Tri Dao — Stanford / Princeton; co-founder. Best known as the author of FlashAttention — one of the most-used pieces of modern transformer infrastructure.
  • Percy Liang — Stanford CS professor. NLP and ML researcher.

The academic credibility of the team is a competitive asset. Customers and partners take Together's technical claims more seriously because the founders are visible researchers.

Original thesis

The original thesis combined several beliefs:

  1. Open-source models will be competitive with closed-source for many use cases.
  2. Inference is a much bigger market than training in dollar terms over time.
  3. Customers want a managed inference platform with per-token pricing, not raw GPU rental.
  4. A platform with strong open-source-model curation, inference optimization, and customer support could capture meaningful share.
  5. The platform should support training and fine-tuning alongside inference — full lifecycle.

Together has executed against each of these. The company's product is per-token inference on curated open-source models plus fine-tuning plus dedicated training infrastructure.

Research lineage

The Together team has continued shipping research, not just product:

  • RedPajama: An open-source reproduction of the LLaMA training dataset. Major credibility-builder in 2023.
  • StripedHyena: Architecture research on alternative attention mechanisms.
  • Sequoia (speculative decoding): Inference acceleration work.
  • Together Research: The internal research arm publishing papers regularly.
  • FlashAttention continues to be improved and integrated.

This research output is unusual for a startup at Together's stage. Most companies focus on shipping product; Together ships both. The research builds credibility and recruits talent.

Funding

Together has raised about $1.3 billion in five announced rounds, by its own blog posts (as of November 2026):

  • $20M seed, announced 15 May 2023.
  • $102.5M Series A led by Kleiner Perkins, 29 November 2023.
  • $106M round led by Salesforce Ventures, 13 March 2024.
  • $305M Series B led by General Catalyst and co-led by Prosperity7, 20 February 2025.
  • $800M Series C, 1 July 2026, with investors including Aramco Ventures, NVIDIA and Vista Equity. Together also reports commitments for over 500 MW of compute capacity, to be financed separately by those new investors.

The company states no valuation in these posts. Investors include strategic AI-focused funds and major institutional investors. The capital base supports both the platform engineering and the infrastructure investments to grow inference capacity.

Scale today

  • Inference API serving production traffic. The Series C post (July 2026) says thousands of customers. The Series B post (February 2025) cited over 450,000 developers.
  • Dedicated training clusters available for customers who need them.
  • GPU footprint via owned hardware and partnerships at substantial scale.
  • Customer base spanning AI startups, enterprises, and platform companies.
  • Revenue is not disclosed in any primary source we fetched. Earlier estimates of a nine-figure run rate are unconfirmed.

Together is the largest pure-play managed-inference platform competing in the open-source-model space.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. The original thesis has five beliefs. Which one carries the weight, and what happens to the business if it fails?

    Show answer

    The belief that open models stay competitive with closed ones. The other four assume customers want to run open models. If closed models pulled clearly ahead, the catalog would lose demand and the per-token cost advantage would not matter. See original thesis.

  2. Why does research output matter commercially to a company that sells per-token inference?

    Show answer

    Serving research lowers the cost of each token, which leaves margin at competitive prices. It also lends credibility with buyers and attracts engineers. See research lineage.

  3. A customer says Together is just a GPU cloud with an API. Why is that the wrong frame when you compare it with CoreWeave?

    Show answer

    Together sells tokens, fine-tuning jobs and endpoints rather than GPU hours, so the right comparison is price per token, quality and latency. Its margin depends on how many tokens each GPU serves, not on how many GPUs it rents out. See scale today.

  4. The founding team pairs an experienced entrepreneur with academic researchers. What does each bring, and what tension does the mix create?

    Show answer

    The entrepreneur brings commercial strategy and the researchers bring credibility and a research pipeline. The tension is that published research is public, so a competitor can adopt it, which means the lasting advantage is the team that keeps producing the next improvement. See founders.

Takeaway

  • Together was founded in 2022 by an entrepreneur and four researchers, and that mix of operating and academic credibility sets its posture.
  • Its thesis has five parts: open models will compete with closed ones, inference will outgrow training, buyers want managed per-token service, a curated and optimized platform can win share, and the platform should cover fine-tuning and training too.
  • It ships research as well as product, including RedPajama, StripedHyena, Sequoia and continuing FlashAttention work.
  • It raised about $1.3 billion across five announced rounds from 2023 to July 2026, which pays for platform engineering and GPU capacity.
  • It is a software platform on top of GPU infrastructure, not a GPU cloud, and revenue is undisclosed.
