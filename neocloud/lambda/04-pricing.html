Section B · Product

Pricing & Commercial

Lambda's list prices sit below hyperscalers and above the marketplaces, and since the November 2026 price list at the upper end of the dedicated neoclouds too. The pricing reflects a dedicated enterprise-tier offering, not a discount play.

On-demand pricing

Lambda's list prices per GPU-hour take effect on 16 November 2026. The hyperscaler and Vast columns are the 2025 estimates and were not rechecked.

At these prices Lambda's H100 is about 40 to 60% below the 2025 hyperscaler estimate and well above the 2025 Vast range. The H100 range runs from $4.59 per GPU-hour on the 8-GPU table to $4.89 on the single-GPU table. The positioning matches the value proposition — better reliability and operational quality than Vast at a price premium; significantly cheaper than hyperscalers.

Against the other dedicated neoclouds Lambda is no longer the cheap option. In November 2026 the list price for an on-demand H100 was $3.90 at Crusoe (rising to $4.50 on 1 December), $3.99 at Together.AI and at RunPod Secure Cloud, $4.50 at Nebius, $4.59 to $4.89 at Lambda and $6.16 at CoreWeave. Only CoreWeave lists higher. The 2025 profile called Lambda "competitive rates rather than aggressive discounting"; the 2026 list makes it a premium dedicated cloud whose pitch has to be fabric, 1-Click Clusters and the enterprise motion rather than price (see the pricing comparison).

Reserved pricing

The current pricing page lists no reserved discounts and says to contact Lambda for reserved capacity at its lowest prices. The 2025 profile quoted these discounts:

  • Monthly reservation: 10-20% off on-demand.
  • Six-month reservation: 25-35% off.
  • Annual: 35-45% off.
  • Multi-year strategic: bespoke pricing further below annual rates.

In 2025 reserved Lambda Cloud H100 capacity could come in at $1.50-2/hour effective, which was genuinely competitive with the larger enterprise neoclouds and significantly below hyperscalers.

Cluster pricing

1-Click Cluster pricing depends on cluster size and duration. From 16 November 2026 the pricing page lists, per GPU-hour, for terms of 2 weeks to 1 year:

  • H100: $6.16 at 16 GPUs, $5.85 at 64 GPUs and $5.54 at 256 GPUs.
  • HGX B200: $9.86 at 16 GPUs, $9.36 at 64 GPUs and $8.87 at 256 or more GPUs.
  • Terms over one year are not priced on the page.

At $5.54 to $6.16 per GPU-hour the committed cluster price sits at CoreWeave's on-demand H100 list ($6.16) and above Together.AI's reserved clusters ($3.19 to $3.69) and Crusoe's on-demand rate ($3.90, $4.50 from December). The 1-Click Cluster sells speed of access on terms of weeks to a year, not the lowest rate; deal terms differ.

vs Competitors

  • vs Vast: Lambda costs more but delivers dedicated reliability; not directly competing.
  • vs RunPod Secure Cloud: RunPod lists an H100 at $3.99 against Lambda's $4.59 to $4.89, so Lambda is about 15 to 20% dearer for the same GPU. Lambda sells the cluster fabric and enterprise motion that RunPod's Secure tier lacks.
  • vs CoreWeave: Lambda is cheaper on list ($4.59 against $6.16) but doesn't have the bespoke-pricing advantage at very large reservation scale, where CoreWeave offers up to 60% off for committed use.
  • vs hyperscalers: about 40-60% cheaper on on-demand H100 against the 2025 hyperscaler estimates.

Enterprise contracts

Lambda offers standard enterprise contract structures:

  • Master service agreements.
  • Custom pricing on volume.
  • SOC 2 Type II attestation and ISO 27001, 27017, 27701 and 22301 certifications, listed on Lambda's Trust Portal (as of November 2026).
  • Dedicated account management for larger customers.
  • Direct technical support during deployments.

The enterprise sales motion is mature enough for Lambda to compete for seven- and eight-figure annual deals.

Where Lambda's pricing is competitive

Lambda is most price-competitive for:

  • Mid-market customers who want a dedicated cloud with a cluster fabric without negotiating with CoreWeave, and who will pay above Together.AI, RunPod and Crusoe for it.
  • Customers wanting on-demand flexibility (Lambda's on-demand product is strong).
  • Customers needing 1-Click Cluster training without multi-year commitments.
  • Customers transitioning from on-prem Lambda hardware to cloud — natural Lambda-to-Lambda relationship.

Takeaway

  • On-demand H100 prices from 16 November 2026 run below hyperscalers and well above Vast, but above every dedicated neocloud in this topic except CoreWeave.
  • Reserved discounts deepen with term, from 10 to 20 percent for a month to 35 to 45 percent for a year, in the 2025 figures the current page no longer lists.
  • 1-Click Cluster prices per GPU above the on-demand level and falling with cluster size, from $6.16 at 16 H100s to $5.54 at 256.
  • Lambda is cheaper than CoreWeave for smaller commitments but lacks bespoke pricing at very large scale.
  • Enterprise terms include master service agreements, volume pricing, a SOC 2 Type II attestation and account management.
  • Every price in the chapter is dated, so confirm against the live price page.
