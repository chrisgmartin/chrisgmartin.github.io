Datacenter footprint

  • Gigawatt-scale contracted power: CoreWeave (over 850 MW active across 43 data centers and about 3.1 GW contracted at the end of 2025), Crusoe (about 1 GW running and over 6 GW contracted, September 2026) and Nebius (a 5 GW contracted-power target for the end of 2026).
  • Smaller or partner-based: Lambda, Together (over 500 MW of commitments announced in July 2026, to be funded by its investors), RunPod Secure Cloud, Hyperbolic.
  • Distributed across many provider sites: Vast, TensorDock, RunPod Community.

Network fabric

  • InfiniBand fabric supporting thousands of GPUs: CoreWeave, Crusoe, Nebius. Also Lambda for cluster offerings.
  • InfiniBand at smaller cluster sizes: Together, RunPod Secure Cloud.
  • Single-node only / no inter-node fabric: Vast, TensorDock, RunPod Community, Hyperbolic marketplace, most consumer-card listings broadly.

Geographic distribution

  • US-anchored, expanding international: CoreWeave, Crusoe.
  • European-anchored, expanding US: Nebius.
  • US + Europe: Lambda, Together, RunPod.
  • Global through providers: Vast, TensorDock.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. A startup needs to train one model across 256 GPUs. Which rows of the fabric section can serve it, and why do the others fail even if the GPUs are the same?

    Show answer

    Only providers with InfiniBand at cluster scale can, namely CoreWeave, Crusoe and Nebius, and Lambda for its cluster offerings. Marketplace and consumer-card supply is single-node, so without a high-bandwidth inter-node fabric gradient exchange stalls and the same GPU count trains far slower. See network fabric.

  2. Why does the chapter call fabric one of the cleanest predictors of which customer workloads a company can serve?

    Show answer

    Fabric decides whether a job can span nodes at all, so it splits training from single-GPU work and inference. It is a hard capability line, while GPU model, price and region are softer. See network fabric.

  3. RunPod lists consumer, workstation and datacenter cards in one product. Who is that mix good for, and who should avoid the consumer tier?

    Show answer

    It suits experiments, fine-tuning and small inference where cost matters and a single node is enough. Teams that need compliance, reliability guarantees or multi-node scaling should use the datacenter tier, since consumer cards lack the memory, fabric and support. See GPU mix.

  4. A European customer with data-residency needs is choosing between CoreWeave and Nebius. What in the chapter bears on the choice?

    Show answer

    Nebius is European-anchored and expanding to the US, while CoreWeave is US-anchored and expanding abroad. Check each provider's actual sites for your workload, because anchoring tells you where it started, not where capacity is free today. See geographic distribution.

Takeaway

  • The datacenter-grade players cluster on H100, H200 and B200, with GB200 and GB300 NVL72 now offered at CoreWeave and Crusoe and Vera Rubin arriving at Nebius and Crusoe.
  • Vast and RunPod offer the broadest card range, including consumer GPUs that the enterprise providers do not sell.
  • Footprint runs from gigawatts of contracted power at CoreWeave, Crusoe and Nebius to capacity spread across many provider sites at the marketplaces.
  • InfiniBand fabric at thousands of GPUs separates the dedicated leaders from single-node marketplace supply.
  • Infrastructure capability tracks capital intensity, and fabric is the cleanest predictor of which workloads a provider can serve.
