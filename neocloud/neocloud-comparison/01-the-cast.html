Reading notes

  • ClusterMAX tiers are from SemiAnalysis ClusterMAX 3.0, published 23 September 2026. SemiAnalysis rated 77 providers in depth. The report covers marketplaces and inference platforms too, and puts Vast.AI, RunPod and Hyperbolic in its Participation Ribbon tier, which sits between Bronze and Underperforming. TensorDock does not appear in the parts of the report open to the public, so its cell stays n/a.
  • "Founded" reflects when the company began operating; some did major pivots later (e.g., Crusoe in 2022-2023).
  • Public/private status as of November 2026.
  • Anchor customers are publicly-known major commitments; many more relationships exist but aren't disclosed.

Companies not covered

The neocloud landscape includes more companies than this set of nine. Notable absences from this guide:

  • Fireworks, Anyscale, Modal, Replicate (managed inference / function platforms not deeply covered).
  • Nscale, smaller European players.
  • TeraWulf, Applied Digital, Iris Energy (smaller energy-led plays).
  • Paperspace and other older specialty providers.
  • Salad, Akash (decentralized compute).
  • Hyperscaler GPU offerings (AWS, Azure, GCP — not technically neoclouds but competitive context).

The nine covered here represent the most commercially important and strategically distinct neocloud-category companies as of 2026.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. A colleague ranks Vast.AI below Nebius because Vast sits in the Participation Ribbon tier and Nebius in Platinum. What is wrong with reading the table that way?

    Show answer

    ClusterMAX grades hands-on cluster quality: reliability, health checks, orchestration, security attestations. A marketplace that resells other hosts' hardware is graded on a different product from a builder that runs its own fleets, so the tiers do not rank businesses. Compare them on model and customer as well. See reading notes.

  2. The table lists Nebius as founded in 2024. Why would that date mislead an investor sizing the company's operating history?

    Show answer

    The date marks the relisting, not the start of operations. Founded reflects when a company began operating and some pivoted later, so check the operating history in the company's filings before using age as a signal. See reading notes.

  3. You are asked which of the nine a small team with a one-week multi-node training job should shortlist. What two columns do you read first, and what do they exclude?

    Show answer

    Read Model and ClusterMAX. Multi-node training needs a cluster fabric, which points to the reserved and on-demand dedicated rows with a higher tier, and rules out the marketplace rows. Then check whether the provider sells at your size. See the summary table.

  4. A founder says the table proves the neocloud market is only nine companies. What does the chapter say about that, and what would you add?

    Show answer

    The nine are the most commercially important and strategically distinct, not the whole market. The chapter names managed-inference platforms, European and energy-led players, older specialists and decentralized networks that it leaves out. Treat the set as a sample of the terrain. See companies not covered.

Takeaway

  • Nine companies span five business models, from reserved-capacity builders to curated marketplaces.
  • In ClusterMAX 3.0 (September 2026) CoreWeave and Nebius are Platinum, Lambda is Silver, Crusoe and Together.AI are Bronze, and the marketplaces sit in the Participation Ribbon tier.
  • Founded dates reflect when operations began and hide pivots such as Crusoe's move from mining to AI.
  • Anchor customers are only the publicly known commitments, so an empty cell does not mean no large customers.
  • The table leaves out inference platforms, European specialists and hyperscaler GPU offerings that still compete for the same buyers.
