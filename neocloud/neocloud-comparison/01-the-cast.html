Companies not covered

The neocloud landscape includes more companies than this set of nine. Notable absences from this guide:

  • Fireworks, Anyscale, Modal, Replicate (managed inference / function platforms not deeply covered).
  • Nscale, smaller European players.
  • TeraWulf, Applied Digital, Iris Energy (smaller energy-led plays).
  • Paperspace and other older specialty providers.
  • Salad, Akash (decentralized compute).
  • Hyperscaler GPU offerings (AWS, Azure, GCP — not technically neoclouds but competitive context).

The nine covered here represent the most commercially important and strategically distinct neocloud-category companies as of 2026.

Takeaway

The cast assembled in this guide spans the strategic terrain of the neocloud industry. The subsequent chapters compare them along specific dimensions.
