Recent Strategic Moves

The 2024-2026 period has seen consequential strategic moves that reshape the landscape. IPOs, multi-billion deals, pivots, and compute futures launches.

2024-2026 strategic moves

  • CoreWeave IPO (2025). Public-market visibility for the Platinum-tier neocloud.
  • Nebius relisting (2024). European-anchored neocloud reaches public markets.
  • CoreWeave Meta deal extensions (2024-2025). Cumulative commitments now in the $35B+ range.
  • Nebius Microsoft deal ($17B+, 2025). Second hyperscaler-anchored neocloud.
  • Stargate announcement (2025). Crusoe and others positioned in the $500B+ initiative.
  • Together fundraising rounds (2024-2025). Continued scaling of the managed-inference category.
  • Lambda Series D ($400M+, 2025). Capital for mid-tier expansion.
  • Compute futures launches (May 2026, ICE following). Financialization of compute.
  • NVIDIA Blackwell volume ramp (2024-2026). Generation rollover affecting all providers.

Implications for the landscape

  • Two clear neocloud tiers (public-market visible Platinum/Gold; private mid-tier).
  • Hyperscaler customer commitments structurally important.
  • Stargate inner circle differentiated from broader category.
  • Compute futures markets enable financial-market participation.
  • Generation rollovers create timing-driven competitive dynamics.
