Recent Strategic Moves

The 2024-2026 period has seen consequential strategic moves that reshape the landscape. IPOs, multi-billion deals, pivots, and compute futures that are still waiting on US regulators.

2024-2026 strategic moves

  • CoreWeave IPO (March 2025). Public-market visibility for the Platinum-tier neocloud. Its stock began trading on Nasdaq on 28 March 2025.
  • Nebius relisting (October 2024). Nasdaq resumed trading in Nebius on 21 October 2024, after it divested its Russian assets.
  • CoreWeave Meta and OpenAI deals (2025-2026). Meta order forms of up to $14.2B (September 2025) and $21.0B (March 2026). OpenAI contracts of up to $11.9B (March 2025) and $6.5B (September 2025). Remaining performance obligations reached $103.7B at 30 June 2026.
  • Nebius Microsoft and Meta deals (2025-2026). About $17.4B from Microsoft (September 2025), then about $3B from Meta (November 2025) and up to $27B from Meta (March 2026).
  • Stargate announcement (2025). Crusoe and others positioned in the $500B initiative OpenAI announced. Crusoe reported over $140B of total contracted value in September 2026.
  • Together $800M Series C (July 2026). Investors include NVIDIA and Salesforce Ventures. Together also reports over 500 MW of compute commitments that its investors will fund separately.
  • Crusoe $3.9B Series F (September 2026). A $30.9B post-money valuation, announced as an initial closing.
  • Lambda Series D, Microsoft and a new chief executive (2025-2026). A $480M Series D (February 2025), a multibillion-dollar Microsoft agreement (November 2025), a Series E of over $1.5B (November 2025) and Michel Combes as chief executive from May 2026.
  • Compute futures announced (May 2026), not yet trading. CME Group and Silicon Data announced H100 and B200 rental index futures on 12 May 2026, and ICE and Ornn followed on 19 May. The CFTC asked for public comment on 19 August, and on 21 September it extended its review of the CME contracts to the end of 9 November 2026. ICE said on 29 September that no listing date was set.
  • NVIDIA Blackwell volume ramp and Vera Rubin (2024-2026). Generation rollover affecting all providers. Nebius received its first Vera Rubin NVL72 systems in 2026 and Crusoe lists Vera Rubin.

Implications for the landscape

  • Two clear neocloud tiers (public-market visible Platinum/Gold; private mid-tier).
  • Hyperscaler customer commitments structurally important.
  • Stargate inner circle differentiated from broader category.
  • Compute futures markets could enable financial-market participation once the CFTC clears them.
  • Generation rollovers create timing-driven competitive dynamics.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. Which two public listings are in the chapter, and what changes for a company when it lists?

    Show answer

    CoreWeave listed in March 2025 and Nebius relisted in October 2024. Listing brings transparency, access to public capital and quarterly pressure, and it lets private peers be compared against audited figures. See 2024 to 2026 strategic moves.

  2. Why does the chapter say hyperscaler commitments are structurally important rather than just large?

    Show answer

    They fund the debt-backed build and underwrite the revenue forecast of the public neoclouds. If those commitments were cut or not renewed, the capital structure would feel it. Size matters less than how much of the business rests on them. See implications.

  3. A private mid-tier neocloud wants to raise on the strength of the Stargate announcement. What separates the inside circle from the rest?

    Show answer

    Participation in contracted buildouts, not the headline figure. The chapter separates the Stargate inner circle from the broader category, so ask whether the company holds a signed role and capacity or only operates in the same market. See implications.

  4. How does a Blackwell volume ramp create timing-driven competition between a neocloud with early supply and one without?

    Show answer

    The early one can sell the newest generation to customers who want it while rivals still hold previous-generation fleets. Rollovers also put older fleets under price pressure, so the order of delivery shifts margins and wins. See 2024 to 2026 strategic moves.

  5. What would compute futures offer a neocloud once they trade, and what would they not change?

    Show answer

    They would let sellers and buyers hedge future capacity and bring financial participants in. They would not change the cost of building capacity or the exposure to GPU price declines on unhedged fleets. See implications.

Takeaway

  • The 2024 to 2026 moves are the CoreWeave IPO, the Nebius relisting, the Meta and Microsoft commitments, Stargate, new funding rounds, compute futures awaiting CFTC clearance and the Blackwell-to-Rubin ramp.
  • The landscape now has two tiers: public-market-visible leaders and a private mid-tier.
  • Hyperscaler commitments are structurally important because they underwrite the debt-funded builds.
  • The Stargate inner circle is a separate group from the broader category.
  • Compute futures would let financial participants and hedgers into a market of bilateral contracts, but as of November 2026 neither CME nor ICE lists them.
  • Generation rollovers make delivery timing a competitive variable.
