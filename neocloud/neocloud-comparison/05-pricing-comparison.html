Fairness adjustments

Direct price comparison is misleading because the products differ:

  • Reliability variance: Marketplace average is meaningfully lower than dedicated.
  • Fabric: Cluster fabric matters for multi-node workloads, available only on dedicated providers.
  • Support and SLAs: Bundled in dedicated; absent in most marketplace.
  • Compliance: SOC 2 etc. available at dedicated providers; not across marketplace supply.
  • Networking, storage: Different ecosystems.

For an apples-to-apples cost, factor these in. A "cheap" Vast instance that requires 1.3x the wall-clock time due to slower DLPerf may not be cheaper end-to-end than a dedicated H100.

Reserved pricing

Reserved discounts off on-demand:

  • CoreWeave: up to 60% off on-demand for committed use. Crusoe and Nebius publish no percentage and sell commitments through sales.
  • Lambda: committed H100 clusters run $5.54 to $6.16 per GPU-hour, above the on-demand rates that take effect on 16 November 2026. Its page publishes no annual discount.
  • Together.AI: reserved GPU clusters step down from $3.99 on-demand to $3.19 for 91 to 180 days, about 20 percent off. Longer terms go through sales.
  • RunPod: reserved clusters of 1 to 12 months or more are quoted by sales, with no published percentage.
  • Vast.AI: reserved offers of 1, 3 or 6 months at up to 50% off. Most other marketplaces have no formal reserved tier.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. A Vast.AI H100 costs $2 an hour and a dedicated H100 costs $3.50. Under what conditions is the dedicated one cheaper for the job?

    Show answer

    When the cheap instance needs more wall-clock time or restarts. At 1.3 times the runtime the $2 box costs $2.60 per unit of work, and an interruptible lost run or a failed host pushes it further. Compare cost per completed job, not per hour. See fairness adjustments.

  2. Why can the same H100 list at $6.16 an hour at CoreWeave and from $2.69 at RunPod Community without either being mispriced?

    Show answer

    The products differ in reliability, fabric, support, compliance and ecosystem. The dedicated price bundles those and the community price strips them out, so the gap prices the bundle. See per-GPU-hour list pricing.

  3. Your CFO wants the lowest listed rate for a year-long production inference service with a SOC 2 requirement. Which rows are out and why?

    Show answer

    The marketplace rows are out, since compliance such as SOC 2 is not available across marketplace supply, and the interruptible tier is unsuitable for production. Compare dedicated providers on reserved annual rates, where the discounts apply. See fairness adjustments.

  4. Which provider publishes the deepest reserved discount, and what does the provider get in return?

    Show answer

    CoreWeave publishes up to 60 percent off on-demand for committed use, and the largest multi-year deals are negotiated beyond any list. The provider gets contracted revenue that supports the debt behind the capacity, so the discount pays for demand certainty. See reserved pricing.

  5. The pricing table is labelled illustrative. How should you use it in a build-or-rent analysis?

The headline spread of about three times, from $6.16 at CoreWeave to $1.99 preemptible at Together.AI, narrows after fairness adjustments, and list prices change at Lambda on 16 November 2026 and at Crusoe on 1 December 2026, so pull current rates from each provider's pricing page for the decision. Takeaway

  • Headline H100 prices in November 2026 vary about three times across the cast, from CoreWeave at the top to preemptible and interruptible capacity at the bottom.
  • A lower hourly rate is not a lower cost per job once reliability, speed, fabric, support and compliance are priced in.
  • Marketplace supply generally has no SLA, no SOC 2 coverage and no cluster fabric, which explains most of the discount.
  • Only CoreWeave (up to 60 percent), Together.AI (about 20 percent at 91 to 180 days) and Vast.AI (up to 50 percent) publish a reserved discount. Lambda, Crusoe, Nebius and RunPod sell commitments through sales.
  • Treat the table as relative positioning, dated November 2026, and check current pricing pages before you decide.
