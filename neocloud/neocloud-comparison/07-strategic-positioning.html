Section C · Markets

Strategic Positioning

Where each company has structural advantage and where they're vulnerable.

Structural advantages by company

  • CoreWeave: Scale, NVIDIA relationship, customer commitments, operational maturity.
  • Crusoe: Energy cost basis, Stargate involvement.
  • Nebius: European positioning, engineering depth, public capital access.
  • Lambda: Brand and customer relationships from hardware heritage, mid-market segment fit.
  • Together.AI: Research credibility, optimization stack, lifecycle product.
  • RunPod: DX investment, two-product flexibility, Serverless offering.
  • Hyperbolic: Founder credibility, dual-product story.
  • Vast.AI: Supply density, marketplace network effects, capital efficiency.
  • TensorDock: Supply curation, customer consistency.

Vulnerabilities by company

  • CoreWeave: Customer concentration, NVIDIA dependency, debt-financed cap-ex risk.
  • Crusoe: Stargate concentration, energy advantage finite, ESG narrative.
  • Nebius: Geopolitical heritage, customer concentration, public-market pressure.
  • Lambda: Scale ceiling, capital constraint, DX gap.
  • Together.AI: Hyperscaler absorption risk, margin compression.
  • RunPod: Sub-scale on both axes vs specialists.
  • Hyperbolic: Sub-scale on both axes, brand recognition gap.
  • Vast.AI: Segment ceiling at enterprise; can't serve compliance-bound customers.
  • TensorDock: Scale vs Vast, brand recognition.

Moat strength (subjective ranking)

From strongest to weakest moat:

  1. CoreWeave (scale + customer commitments + NVIDIA).
  2. Crusoe (energy + Stargate inner-circle).
  3. Nebius (engineering + European positioning + public-market capital).
  4. Vast.AI (supply density + marketplace network).
  5. Lambda (brand + segment fit).
  6. Together.AI (research + optimization stack).
  7. RunPod (DX + product breadth).
  8. Hyperbolic (founder credibility + dual product).
  9. TensorDock (curation vs competitors).

Takeaway

The strategic positions are coherent within each company's segment but vulnerable at the edges. The next chapter examines the recent strategic moves reshaping the landscape.
