Risk-return shape

  • High return, high risk: CoreWeave, Crusoe, Nebius. Big upside if execution; big downside if customer concentration or capital structure breaks.
  • Moderate return, moderate risk: Lambda, Together. Less explosive but more defensible.
  • Modest return, low-to-moderate risk: RunPod, Hyperbolic, Vast, TensorDock. Bounded upside; capital-efficient business models.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. The matrix puts CoreWeave, Crusoe and Nebius in high return, high risk. What single condition turns the bull case into the bear case for all three?

    Show answer

    A break in customer concentration or the capital structure. They rely on a small set of buyers and debt-funded capacity, so a lost or renegotiated commitment hits revenue and debt service together. See risk-return shape.

  2. Lambda's bull case includes IPO upside and its bear case says squeezed by both ends. Explain both ends.

    Show answer

    Above it, CoreWeave-scale players compete for larger customers. Below it, marketplaces and cheaper dedicated providers compete on price. The capital ceiling stops it from moving up, so brand has to hold the middle. See thesis matrix.

  3. The matrix calls Vast.AI modest return and low risk. What would invalidate the bull case?

    Show answer

    GPU price normalization compresses the value proposition of cheap rental, and the segment ceiling stops growth into enterprise. A fall in H100 prices at dedicated providers narrows the gap marketplaces sell on. See thesis matrix.

  4. You hold a view that hyperscalers will grow internal capacity quickly. Which cells in the matrix does that move, and in which direction?

    Show answer

    It weakens the bull cases for CoreWeave, Nebius and Crusoe, whose backlog rests on hyperscaler buying, and strengthens the concentration bear case. It matters less for marketplaces, which do not depend on hyperscaler commitments. See thesis matrix.

  5. The chapter says to treat the matrix as a starting framework rather than a verdict. What would you add before relying on it?

    Show answer

    Current financials from filings, contract terms and renewal dates, utilization and price trends, and the debt schedule. The matrix gives bull and bear in a line each, and a decision needs the numbers behind each claim. See risk-return shape.

Takeaway

  • Every company has a short bull and a short bear case, and the matrix is a framework and not a verdict.
  • CoreWeave, Crusoe and Nebius carry the highest upside and the highest risk because of customer concentration and debt-funded growth.
  • Lambda and Together sit in the middle: more defensible, less explosive, and exposed to squeeze or absorption.
  • RunPod, Hyperbolic, Vast and TensorDock have bounded upside and capital-efficient models.
  • No single thesis applies across the cast, so match the thesis to the model and the exposure.
