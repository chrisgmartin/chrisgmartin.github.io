Hyperscaler vs Neocloud Dynamics

The coopetition dynamic — hyperscalers as both customers and competitors of neoclouds — is the defining feature of the industry's competitive structure.

Coopetition

The standard tech-industry coopetition dynamic at unusual intensity:

  • Hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Google) operate their own GPU compute and compete with neoclouds for enterprise customers.
  • Same hyperscalers buy capacity from neoclouds at very large scale.
  • Some customers (OpenAI, etc.) buy from both simultaneously.
  • The competitive lines shift over time based on capacity, demand, and strategic posture.

Why hyperscalers buy

Several reasons hyperscalers source from neoclouds even while building internally:

  1. Speed. Building new datacenter capacity takes 18-24 months. Buying from a neocloud who already has it is faster.
  2. Capital structure. Neoclouds absorb GPU depreciation risk on the neocloud's balance sheet.
  3. Allocation access. Neocloud's NVIDIA relationships sometimes deliver capacity faster than hyperscaler-direct procurement.
  4. Customer pass-through. Customers (OpenAI for Microsoft, similar) drive demand that the hyperscaler may need to fulfill faster than internal builds allow.
  5. Strategic optionality. Reduces dependence on the hyperscaler's own datacenter build pace.

Why they also build

Hyperscalers continue heavy internal capacity investment because:

  1. Long-term cost advantage of owned capacity.
  2. Strategic independence from third-party suppliers.
  3. Custom silicon advantages (Microsoft's Maia, Google's TPU, AWS's Trainium).
  4. Integration with the broader cloud stack.
  5. Scale that no single neocloud can match.

The shifting balance

The buy-vs-build balance is dynamic. Through 2025-2026, hyperscalers have been buying heavily because internal capacity hadn't caught up to AI demand. As internal capacity ramps, the buy share may decline.

This dynamic is the biggest single risk to neoclouds that depend on hyperscaler commitments. CoreWeave's customer concentration is exposed to this.

Takeaway
