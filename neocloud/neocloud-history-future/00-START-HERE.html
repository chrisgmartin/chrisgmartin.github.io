Scope & audience

The industry-level deep dive. Covers the history (pre-AI through 2026), the business-model taxonomy across players, the hyperscaler-vs-neocloud co-opetition dynamic, NVIDIA's pivotal role, and forward-looking scenarios. Read this before the company guides if you want to start from context; read after if you want to consolidate what you learned.

Key framings to carry

  1. The category is younger than it looks. 'Neocloud' as a term and a coherent industry is really a 2023–2024 phenomenon. The companies existed earlier; the category framing didn't.
  2. Co-opetition with hyperscalers is the defining dynamic. Microsoft / Google / Amazon are simultaneously the biggest customers, the biggest competitors, and the biggest threats. Reading neocloud strategy without that frame misses the point.
  3. NVIDIA shapes the market more than any single neocloud does. Allocation, pricing, reference designs, strategic investment — NVIDIA's decisions ripple through the entire category.
