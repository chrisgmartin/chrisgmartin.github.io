The 2025–2026 Enterprise Era

By 2025-2026, the neocloud category had matured into an enterprise infrastructure tier. Hyperscaler customers committed tens of billions over multi-year terms; the Stargate initiative pushed AI compute infrastructure to a generational scale; public listings (CoreWeave, Nebius) brought transparency.

Hyperscaler-tier commitments

The defining feature of 2025-2026 was the scale of hyperscaler commitments to neoclouds:

  • CoreWeave's Microsoft deals at $10B+.
  • CoreWeave's Meta deals reaching ~$35B total in announced and extended commitments through 2032.
  • Nebius's Microsoft commitment at $17B+.
  • Crusoe's Stargate-tied buildouts at potentially $100B+ exposure.

Aggregated, the hyperscaler commitments to neoclouds run into the hundreds of billions. This is a fundamentally different category than the 2022-2023 startup ecosystem.

Stargate

The Stargate initiative (OpenAI / Oracle / SoftBank / MGX / Microsoft) at $500B+ committed scale through the decade marked AI compute infrastructure as a generational investment. Multiple neoclouds participate in Stargate-related buildouts.

Stargate's scope dwarfs any individual neocloud and reshapes the entire AI compute industry's capital structure.

Public listings

Several neoclouds reached public markets:

  • CoreWeave IPO in 2025 on Nasdaq under CRWV.
  • Nebius relisting on Nasdaq under NBIS in late 2024.
  • Speculation about additional IPOs from private neoclouds.

Public listings bring transparency, capital, and pressure. Private peers face both opportunity and tougher competitive comparisons.

The shape of the enterprise era

By 2025-2026, the neocloud landscape has the following shape:

  • A small number of mega-scale players (CoreWeave, Crusoe, Nebius) with hyperscaler-tier commitments.
  • A larger group of mid-tier enterprise neoclouds (Lambda, others) serving mid-market AI demand.
  • Marketplace and inference platforms (Vast, RunPod, Together, Hyperbolic, TensorDock) serving developer and startup demand.
  • Hyperscalers participating as both buyers and sellers in a coopetition dynamic.
  • Compute futures markets emerging to financialize the underlying commodity.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. What makes the 2025 to 2026 commitments a different category from the 2022 to 2023 startup ecosystem?

    Show answer

    Hyperscaler customers committed tens of billions over multi-year terms, which funds debt-backed capacity and turns neoclouds into infrastructure suppliers. Earlier demand came from startups buying hourly. See hyperscaler-tier commitments.

  2. Stargate is described as dwarfing any neocloud. What follows for a neocloud that takes part in it?

    Show answer

    It gains a very large contracted build, and it ties its fortunes to one initiative and its execution. Participation raises both the upside and the concentration. See Stargate.

  3. What does a public listing change for a neocloud, and for its private peers?

    Show answer

    Transparency, access to public capital and quarterly pressure for the listed company. Private peers face tougher comparisons against audited figures while keeping the freedom to stay opaque. See public listings.

  4. Describe the shape of the enterprise-era market in one sentence per tier.

    Show answer

    A few mega-scale players hold hyperscaler-tier commitments. A mid-tier of enterprise neoclouds serves mid-market demand. Marketplaces and inference platforms serve developers and startups. See the shape of the enterprise era.

  5. Why does the chapter use the word coopetition for hyperscalers and neoclouds?

    Show answer

    Hyperscalers buy large capacity from neoclouds while competing for the same enterprise customers. They are customer and competitor at once. See the shape of the enterprise era.

Takeaway

  • The defining feature of 2025 and 2026 is hyperscaler-tier commitments: Microsoft and Meta at CoreWeave, Microsoft at Nebius, Stargate-tied builds at Crusoe.
  • Aggregate hyperscaler commitments to neoclouds run into the hundreds of billions.
  • Stargate sits at $500B+ and reshapes the capital structure of AI compute.
  • CoreWeave and Nebius are listed on Nasdaq, which brings transparency, capital and quarterly pressure.
  • The market has three tiers: mega-scale builders, mid-tier enterprise neoclouds, and marketplace and inference platforms.
