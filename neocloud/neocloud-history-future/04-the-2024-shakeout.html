Section B · History

The 2024 Shake-Out

By 2024 the neocloud category had clearly differentiated into tiers. The Platinum-tier (CoreWeave) pulled ahead on customer commitments and infrastructure scale; Gold-tier players (Crusoe, Nebius) established credible positions; smaller players consolidated or fell out of competitive contention.

Tier emergence

Several dynamics drove the 2024 stratification:

  • Customer commitments concentrated at the best-operating, largest-scale providers.
  • NVIDIA allocation patterns rewarded scale and strategic alignment.
  • Capital availability favored already-funded leaders.
  • Operational complexity at scale created barriers to entry.

ClusterMAX publication

SemiAnalysis's ClusterMAX 1.0 (March 2025) and then 2.0 (November 2025) ratings gave the industry a vocabulary for the hierarchy that the 2024 shake-out had produced. The framework's specific tier names (Platinum, Gold, Silver) became commonly used. CoreWeave's sole occupancy of the Platinum tier crystallized in public consciousness. That ended in September 2026, when ClusterMAX 3.0 added Nebius to Platinum and moved Crusoe down to Bronze.

Platinum divergence

CoreWeave's gap to second place widened through 2024:

  • The Microsoft and Meta deals at scales no competitor could match.
  • Earlier B200 deployments than competitors.
  • Operational sophistication validated by sustained customer relationships.
  • IPO preparation that culminated in the 2025 public listing.

Also-rans

Some companies that aspired to neocloud status didn't reach the level of credibility the Gold-tier players achieved:

  • Some companies couldn't raise sufficient capital and remained sub-scale.
  • Some couldn't deliver operationally at the standards customers expected.
  • Some lost NVIDIA allocation as the competitive landscape shifted.
  • Some pivoted to adjacent businesses (managed inference, specialized verticals).

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. List the dynamics that sorted neoclouds into tiers in 2024, and say which one reinforces the others.

    Show answer

    Customer commitments concentrated at the best operators, NVIDIA allocation rewarded scale, capital favored funded leaders and operations at scale raised barriers. Capital and commitments reinforce each other, because a funded leader wins commitments that unlock more debt. See tier emergence.

  2. How did ClusterMAX change the competitive picture beyond rating providers?

    Show answer

    It gave the industry tier names and a shared vocabulary, and it made CoreWeave's sole Platinum position public. Buyers and investors began to use the tiers as shorthand. See ClusterMAX publication.

  3. List three ways CoreWeave pulled ahead of second place in 2024.

    Show answer

    Microsoft and Meta deals at a scale no competitor matched, earlier B200 deployments, operations validated by sustained customer relationships, and IPO preparation. See Platinum divergence.

  4. A founder says the neoclouds that fell out of contention just lacked good engineers. What does the chapter say?

    Show answer

    Capital, operations and NVIDIA allocation all mattered. Some could not raise enough, some could not deliver to customer standards, some lost allocation and some pivoted into adjacent businesses. See also-rans.

Takeaway

  • By 2024 commitments, NVIDIA allocation and capital concentrated at the largest and best-run providers.
  • ClusterMAX 1.0 (March 2025) and 2.0 (November 2025) gave the industry its Platinum, Gold and Silver vocabulary.
  • CoreWeave widened its lead through bigger deals, earlier B200 deployments and IPO preparation.
  • Gold-tier players such as Crusoe and Nebius established credible positions. Lambda was rated Silver in 2.0.
  • Smaller entrants fell short on capital, operations or allocation, or pivoted into adjacent businesses.
