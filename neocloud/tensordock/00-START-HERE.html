Scope & audience

TensorDock as a marketplace alternative to Vast — what's similar, what's different in supply curation, product surface, pricing, customer segments, and outlook. A useful case study in marketplace differentiation at sub-Vast scale.

Key framings to carry

  1. Curation is the differentiation lever. Vast has the largest supply; TensorDock bets on slightly more curated supply with more consistent quality. The tradeoff is volume vs reliability.
  2. The founder story is unusual. Bootstrap roots, founded young. That shapes the company culture and the strategic timeline.
  3. Scale ceiling is the question. Marketplaces have network effects. Whether TensorDock can build enough supply density to challenge Vast on its own terms is the strategic open question.
