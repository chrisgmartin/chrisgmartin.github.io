Strengths & Weaknesses

TensorDock's strengths cluster around curation and consistency; weaknesses around scale and brand recognition relative to Vast.

Strength: curation

The supply curation produces a more consistently reliable rental experience than Vast's average.

Strength: customer experience consistency

Customers know what they're getting more predictably than at Vast.

Weakness: scale vs Vast

The smaller supply pool means thinner liquidity. Customers sometimes can't find what they want.

Weakness: brand

Smaller mind-share than Vast. Many potential customers don't know TensorDock exists.

Takeaway

TensorDock's strengths and weaknesses are coherent. The next chapter places the company head-to-head.
