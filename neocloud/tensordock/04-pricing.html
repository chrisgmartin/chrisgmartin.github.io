Pricing & Commercial

TensorDock's pricing is positioned between Vast (cheapest) and RunPod Community (more polished). The curation premium translates to roughly 10-30% above Vast for the same nominal GPU.

On-demand pricing

Indicative on-demand pricing approximately:

  • RTX 4090: $0.35-0.50/hour
  • A100 80GB: $1.20-1.60/hour
  • H100 80GB: $2.30-3.00/hour

Reserved pricing

Reserved discounts available for longer commitments.

vs Vast pricing

TensorDock prices 10-30% above Vast for the same nominal GPU. The premium reflects the curation and slightly more consistent quality. Customers willing to pay the small premium for reliability favor TensorDock.
