Section B · Operations

Pricing & Commercial

TensorDock's pricing is positioned between Vast (cheapest) and RunPod Community (more polished). The curation premium translates to roughly 10-30% above Vast for the same nominal GPU.

On-demand pricing

The cloud GPUs page lists these "from" prices per GPU-hour as of November 2026. Hosts set their own prices, so the same GPU has several prices. The table on that page is stamped July 2024, and the live dashboard rules:

  • RTX 4090 24GB: from $0.35/hour
  • A100 80GB: from $1.50/hour (PCIe) and $1.80/hour (SXM4)
  • H100 SXM5 80GB: from $2.25/hour, promoted as the lowest H100 price in the industry

Reserved pricing

Reserved discounts are available for longer commitments. The site gives no rate and asks you to contact sales (as of November 2026).

vs Vast pricing

TensorDock prices 10-30% above Vast for the same nominal GPU. The premium reflects the curation and slightly more consistent quality. Customers willing to pay the small premium for reliability favor TensorDock.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. Vast lists an A100 at $1.00 an hour. Using the chapter's premium, what would TensorDock charge, and what does the extra money buy?

    Show answer

    It would charge $1.10 to $1.30. The difference pays for curated hosts, continuous monitoring and steadier performance, which lower the buyer's expected cost of interruptions. See vs Vast pricing.

  2. A ten-hour job costs $1.00 an hour on Vast, which fails at hour 8 with probability 0.2 and restarts from scratch. TensorDock charges $1.25 an hour and never fails. Which is cheaper in expectation, and what would change the answer?

    Show answer

    Vast costs 10 + 0.2 × 8 = 11.6 hours, or $11.60, against $12.50 on TensorDock, so Vast still wins. TensorDock breaks even when the failure chance reaches about 31%. Longer jobs, later failures, deadlines or no checkpointing push toward the premium. See vs Vast pricing.

  3. Your finance team wants to budget a year of H100 rental from the "from" price on the site. What is wrong with that, and what do you do instead?

    Show answer

    The figures are floor prices for a marketplace whose prices move with supply and host choice. Take a live quote at deploy time, budget with a margin, and price a reserved commitment if usage is steady. See on-demand pricing.

  4. Why would a marketplace offer a reserved discount at all?

    Show answer

    A commitment gives the platform and its hosts predictable utilisation, which lets hosts cover fixed costs and plan capacity. The discount pays for that predictability. See reserved pricing.

Takeaway

  • TensorDock sits between Vast, the cheapest, and RunPod Community, the more polished option.
  • It charges roughly 10 to 30% more than Vast for the same nominal GPU, and the premium pays for curation.
  • The site's "from" on-demand rates as of November 2026 are $0.35 an hour for an RTX 4090, $1.50 for an A100 80GB PCIe ($1.80 for SXM4) and $2.25 for an H100 SXM5. They are floors, not quotes.
  • Reserved discounts reward longer commitments.
  • Customers who pay the premium are buying steadier delivery of the same GPU, not more capability.
