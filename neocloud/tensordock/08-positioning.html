Section D · Position

Competitive Positioning

TensorDock is the most direct Vast comparable in the marketplace category. RunPod Community Cloud and longer-tail competitors like Salad / Akash complete the competitive landscape.

vs Vast

The defining comparison. Vast has scale; TensorDock has curation. Both can coexist; customer preference axis determines which wins each specific transaction.

vs RunPod Community

RunPod Community Cloud has better DX and the Secure Cloud bridge. TensorDock has a simpler product surface and is sometimes slightly cheaper.

vs Salad / Akash

Salad (consumer-device-based compute) and Akash (decentralized compute) operate in adjacent but different categories. Less direct competition for TensorDock's mainstream marketplace.

Takeaway

TensorDock's position is real but constrained. The next chapter examines financial shape.
