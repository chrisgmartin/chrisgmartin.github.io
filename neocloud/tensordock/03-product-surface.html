Section B · Operations

Product Surface

TensorDock offers pods, dedicated servers, and standard cloud-style management. The product is functional and comparable to other marketplace competitors.

Pods and instances

The primary product is on-demand GPU instances launchable through the web UI or API. Pre-built templates support common workloads.

Dedicated servers

For longer-term commitments, dedicated server rental is available. Providers reserve specific hardware for a customer for the contract duration.

On-demand vs reserved

Like other marketplaces, on-demand and reserved tiers exist. Reserved pricing offers meaningful discounts.

Developer experience

The DX is functional but not as polished as RunPod's. Web UI is clean; CLI/API for automation; documentation is adequate.

Takeaway

The product surface is workmanlike. The next chapter covers pricing.
