Section D · Position

Customers

TensorDock's customer base overlaps heavily with Vast's: indie ML, students, small teams, cost-sensitive workloads.

Customer segments

  • Indie ML developers.
  • Students and researchers.
  • Small AI companies with non-production workloads.
  • Some inference-platform customers using TensorDock as a backend.

Why TensorDock

Customers pick TensorDock over Vast when they value the slightly-more-curated supply enough to justify the small price premium. Customers pick TensorDock over RunPod when they prefer marketplace pricing over the slightly higher RunPod premium.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. Why is an inference platform that uses TensorDock as a backend worth more than a student renting one GPU, and what must TensorDock deliver to keep it?

    Show answer

    The platform brings steady, recurring spend. It also passes failures on to its own customers, so it needs predictable availability and performance, which is what curation is meant to supply. See customer segments.

  2. Describe the buyer who picks TensorDock over Vast, and the buyer who picks it over RunPod.

    Show answer

    The first values curated supply enough to pay a small premium over Vast's lowest prices. The second prefers marketplace pricing and a simple product to RunPod's higher premium. See why TensorDock.

  3. The chapter lists small companies with non-production workloads. What changes when such a company starts serving paying users in production?

    Show answer

    Production needs uptime commitments, support response times, often compliance and failover across sites. A marketplace with variable hosts rarely supplies all of that, so the customer tends to move to a larger provider. See customer segments.

  4. The base is price-sensitive. What does that mean for retention, and what keeps these customers from leaving?

    Show answer

    Switching costs are low, so they leave for a cheaper offer the moment the experience slips. Retention rests on delivered reliability rather than lock-in, so one bad stretch sends them back to Vast. See why TensorDock.

Takeaway

  • The customer base overlaps heavily with Vast's: indie ML developers, students and researchers, and small AI companies with non-production workloads.
  • Some inference platforms use TensorDock as a backend.
  • Customers choose TensorDock over Vast when they value curated supply enough to pay a small premium.
  • Customers choose it over RunPod when they prefer marketplace pricing and a simpler product.
  • The base looks like Vast's at a smaller scale, with the same price sensitivity.
