Eleven-chapter profile of RunPod — the most direct competitor to Vast.AI's marketplace side, while also operating a dedicated 'Secure Cloud' for enterprise. The two-product model is the strategic crux.

Scope & audience

RunPod as a company, its dual product offering (Community Cloud and Secure Cloud), pricing, infrastructure, customers, and competitive positioning against Vast, Lambda, and CoreWeave.

Key framings to carry

  1. Two products, one company. Community Cloud is a marketplace; Secure Cloud is reserved dedicated. They're separate offerings with separate economics and customer segments. Understanding both is the key insight.
  2. Developer experience is the wedge. RunPod's pod model, templates, and serverless flavor differentiate from Vast's raw SSH-onto-a-machine. That UX is the moat against marketplace competitors.
  3. Capital is the constraint. Secure Cloud requires capital to buy GPUs; Community Cloud doesn't. The strategic question is how much capital RunPod can deploy without becoming a CoreWeave-shaped business.

Reading order

01 (the company) → 02 (two-product model — load-bearing) → 03 (product surface) → 04 (pricing) → 05–07 (operational details) → 08–10 (strengths, weaknesses, positioning, outlook). Each chapter is short; aim for ~90 minutes end-to-end.
