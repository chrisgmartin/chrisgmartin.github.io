Eleven-chapter profile of Crusoe — the energy-arbitrage neocloud. Captures stranded gas to power AI compute. Pivoted entirely from Bitcoin mining to AI. Major OpenAI-adjacent buildouts.

Scope & audience

Crusoe's energy thesis, the pivot from crypto to AI, datacenter buildouts (Permian, Wyoming, Iceland), commercial model, ESG narrative, and financial shape. Where energy-arbitrage fits among neocloud business models.

Key framings to carry

  1. Energy is the moat, not compute. Crusoe's structural advantage is power sourcing — flare gas, stranded power, behind-the-meter siting. The GPUs are commodity; the cost-of-electricity is what differentiates.
  2. The pivot story matters. Going all-in on AI in 2022–2023 timed brilliantly. The Bitcoin-mining heritage is both an asset (datacenter operations expertise) and a narrative liability (ESG questions).
  3. Scale is the bet. Stargate-adjacent deals signal Crusoe positioning as a frontier-lab compute supplier. That's a different game from Vast / RunPod / Lambda.
