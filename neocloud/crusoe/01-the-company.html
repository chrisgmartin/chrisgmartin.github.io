Section A · Origin & scale

The Company

Crusoe was founded in 2018 on an unusual thesis: stranded gas at oil wells could power compute. The company started in Bitcoin mining, pivoted hard to AI in 2022-2023, and now operates some of the most consequential AI-compute buildouts in the industry.

Founding (2018)

Crusoe (then Crusoe Energy Systems) was founded in 2018 by Chase Lochmiller and Cully Cavness. The Denver-based company began as an energy-and-compute play: the founders had spotted that oil and gas operators in places like the Permian Basin were flaring (burning off) natural gas because it wasn't economic to pipe it to market.

The proposition was simple: take the flare gas, run it through a generator on-site, and use the electricity to power computing equipment. The compute pays for itself (and the gas), the gas doesn't go to waste, and the carbon footprint is meaningfully better than letting the gas flare uncontrolled.

Original thesis

The three legs of the original thesis:

  1. Stranded energy is structurally cheaper than grid power. The energy producer values it at zero or negative; capturing it costs less than buying grid electricity.
  2. Computing demand is large and growing. Whatever demand sink — initially crypto mining; later AI compute — there's always a buyer for cheap GPU-hours.
  3. The combination creates a moat. Competitors paying grid prices can't match the energy cost basis. The advantage compounds over time as more sites come online.

This is fundamentally an energy-arbitrage thesis with compute as the demand sink. The choice of compute (Bitcoin in 2018-2022; AI in 2022 onward) was secondary to the energy story.

The crypto-to-AI pivot

Through 2018-2022, Crusoe's primary compute workload was Bitcoin mining. The economics worked: cheap flare-gas-powered electricity, ASIC-based mining hardware, steady Bitcoin block rewards.

In 2022 the pivot to AI began. Drivers:

  • Bitcoin's post-halving economics were tightening.
  • The Ethereum proof-of-stake transition (Sept 2022) eliminated a large GPU-mining demand sink.
  • ChatGPT's November 2022 launch made AI compute demand a credible long-term thesis.
  • GPU pricing was high but the time-to-payback math worked given the energy cost advantage.

By 2023, Crusoe was building AI-compute capacity aggressively. By 2024-2025, the company's narrative had fully transformed — Crusoe is described in press as an AI infrastructure company rather than an energy-arbitrage company. Crusoe sold its Bitcoin mining business to NYDIG in 2025 to focus on AI infrastructure (company timeline, as of November 2026).

Founders

  • Chase Lochmiller — CEO and chairman. Background spanning investment management and energy. Drives the strategic narrative and commercial relationships.
  • Cully Cavness — President and Chief Strategy Officer. Background in oil and gas. Drives the energy-side relationships and operational deployment.

The founder combination of energy and finance is well-matched to a business that's simultaneously building datacenters in oil patches and raising capital from sophisticated investors.

Funding history

Crusoe has raised substantial capital across multiple rounds:

  • Early rounds in 2019-2020 from energy-focused and tech-focused investors.
  • Several rounds during the 2021-2022 crypto cycle scaled the early business.
  • Major funding rounds in 2023-2024 supported the AI pivot and aggressive capacity buildouts.
  • Series D in December 2024: $600M at a $2.8B valuation, led by Founders Fund.
  • Series E in October 2025: $1.375B at a valuation above $10B, co-led by Valor Equity Partners and Mubadala Capital.
  • Series F announced in September 2026: an anticipated $3.9B at $30.9B post-money, co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners.
  • The disclosed rounds from Series B to Series F total about $6.4B, counting the Series E and F amounts as announced, plus significant debt.

Investors include strategic energy companies, traditional VCs, and infrastructure-focused funds. The capital base is large enough to fund multi-hundred-MW buildouts.

Scale today

Crusoe's 2025-2026 scale signals:

  • About 1 GW of gross capacity delivered and operating, and over 6 GW of gross contracted capacity (Crusoe, September 2026).
  • No GPU count is published.
  • Crusoe cites $140B+ in total contracted value, tied to large-scale AI training and cloud customers including the Stargate campus at Abilene.
  • Bronze in SemiAnalysis ClusterMAX 3.0 (September 2026), down from Gold in 2.0. CoreWeave and Nebius are Platinum.
  • Over 1,800 employees across five countries (Crusoe, September 2026).

The buildout pace through 2025-2026 has been one of the fastest in the industry. Crusoe is one of a small handful of companies operating at the scale needed to handle frontier model training.

✓ Check yourself

Answer from memory before opening each one.

  1. What was the original Crusoe thesis, and why was the choice of Bitcoin as the first compute load secondary to it?

    Show answer

    Stranded energy costs less than grid power, so compute placed next to it has a lower cost basis than rivals paying grid prices. The compute was only the demand sink for that energy, so it could change from Bitcoin to AI without changing the thesis. See the original thesis.

  2. Name two things that pushed Crusoe from mining towards AI around 2022, and one that made it credible.

    Show answer

    Tightening Bitcoin economics and the Ethereum move to proof of stake in September 2022 removed demand for GPU mining. The ChatGPT launch in November 2022 made AI demand a believable long-term bet. See the pivot.

  3. Why is flare gas a good power source for a compute load, from the point of view of the oil producer?

    Show answer

    The producer cannot economically pipe the gas to market, so it values it at zero or less, and capturing it reduces a regulatory and reputational liability from flaring. See the founding.

  4. Crusoe is described as an AI infrastructure company today. What evidence in the chapter supports that, and what remains of the old business?

    Show answer

    The 2023 to 2025 buildouts, the Stargate-adjacent customer commitments and the press framing. Crusoe sold its Bitcoin mining business to NYDIG in 2025. See the pivot.

  5. A peer says Crusoe's ClusterMAX Gold rating shows it matches CoreWeave. What is wrong with that reading?

    Show answer

    Gold sat one tier below CoreWeave's Platinum in the 2.0 ratings, and in ClusterMAX 3.0 (September 2026) Crusoe fell to Bronze, while CoreWeave and Nebius hold Platinum. Crusoe's fleet is also smaller. See scale today.

Takeaway

  • Crusoe started in 2018 as an energy-arbitrage business, turning flared gas into power for compute.
  • The thesis is cost: stranded energy undercuts grid prices, and compute is the buyer of that energy.
  • Crypto weakness and the AI surge in 2022 and 2023 moved the company from mining to AI infrastructure.
  • Funding rounds from 2019 onward total about $6.4 billion of announced equity through the September 2026 Series F, plus debt.
  • Scale is large and growing, but Crusoe is rated Bronze in ClusterMAX 3.0, well below CoreWeave's Platinum.
